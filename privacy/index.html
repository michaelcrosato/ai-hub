Privacy

Last changed 27 October 2026.

The AI Hub is a private arcade of browser games. You can play every game without an account. Signing in is only for rating games, keeping your play history across devices, and hosting a session with friends.

Without an account

Your favourites, play history and settings stay in this browser (local storage). Nothing about you is sent anywhere. Clearing the site's data forgets them.

When you sign in with Google

  • Your Google account id and email address, kept by our sign-in provider (Supabase) to recognise you. The hub asks Google for nothing else and keeps none of Google's own tokens.
  • The session nickname you pick from the hub's list ("Teal Otter"). Your Google name and photo are never shown to anyone.
  • Your thumbs up or down on each game you rate.
  • Which games you played, when, and for how long they were open in front of you.
  • The results of sessions you take part in: who placed where, and the points.

Only you can read your votes and play history. The site's owner can read them in the database, to see which games people like.

Guests in a session

Joining a friend's session without Google makes an anonymous guest account in this browser: a random id and the nickname you pick. Guests cannot rate games and have no play history. Guest accounts unused for 30 days are deleted.

Playing with friends

To keep games quick, the browsers in a session connect directly to each other where they can, which lets each player's browser see the others' internet (IP) addresses, as with most online games. Where a direct connection is not possible, traffic goes through a relay (Cloudflare) and the addresses stay hidden. There is no chat: players only see each other's nicknames, colours and game moves.

Deleting your data

Signed in, open the account menu and choose Delete my data. That deletes your account, your votes, your play history and your session results at once. You may be asked to sign in again first.

Questions

Ask whoever invited you to the AI Hub; they know its owner.
