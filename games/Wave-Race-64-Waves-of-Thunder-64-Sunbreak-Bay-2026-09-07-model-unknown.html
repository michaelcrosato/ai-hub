RIDER'S HANDBOOK

FEEL THE WATER.

Hold the throttle. Lean into each turn. Release the throttle or brake for tight turns. Waves lift your craft and change its grip.

RED → PASS RIGHTKeep the buoy on your left.
YELLOW ← PASS LEFTKeep the buoy on your right.

Each correct buoy adds power. A missed buoy removes all power. Five misses end the race. Stay inside the pink markers. Use the light-blue guide to find the next turn. A clean lap can set a best time and save a time-trial ghost. Hit the throttle during the final count of 1 for a perfect start.

Two courses: Sunbreak Bay (12 buoys) and Lighthouse Point (14 buoys), which runs the other way round the bay, under the middle of the bridge and through the channel inside the lighthouse. The Thunder Cup races both back to back against three rivals for 7, 4, 2 and 1 points.

Choose an engine before you start: 750 cc is standard, 1125 cc adds 50% speed, and 1500 cc adds 100%. All riders use the selected engine class. Wave size and weather are separate choices. Large and Extreme waves lift the craft higher. Blue Storm adds heavy rain, a dark blue sky, lightning, and thunder. Best laps, race times and ghosts are saved for each course and setup.
