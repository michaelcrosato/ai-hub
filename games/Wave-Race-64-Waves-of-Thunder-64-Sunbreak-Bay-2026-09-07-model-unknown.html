RIDER'S HANDBOOK

FEEL THE WATER.

Hold the throttle. Lean into each turn. Release the throttle or brake for tight turns. Waves lift your craft and change its grip.

RED → PASS RIGHTKeep the buoy on your left.
YELLOW ← PASS LEFTKeep the buoy on your right.

Each correct buoy adds power. A missed buoy removes all power. Five misses end the race. Stay inside the pink markers. Use the light-blue guide to find the next turn. A clean lap can set a best time and save a time-trial ghost.

Choose an engine before you start: 750 cc is standard, 1125 cc adds 50% speed, and 1500 cc adds 100%. All riders use the selected engine class. Wave size and weather are separate choices. Large and Extreme waves lift the craft higher. Blue Storm adds heavy rain, a dark blue sky, lightning, and thunder. Best laps and ghosts are saved for each setup.

MENU GAMEPAD: LB changes the engine · RB changes wave size · Y changes weather.

GAMEPAD: left stick steers · RT / A accelerates · LT / B brakes · X jumps · Y changes camera · Start pauses. Touch controls appear on touch screens.
