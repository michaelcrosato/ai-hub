Coyote flight school

A little reckless. Very effective.

01 / Fly & fire

Left stick / WASD or arrows to fly. Hold R2 or ✕ / Space for twin lasers. Line up the pale green flight sight with a target. Gentle aim assist helps.

02 / Get a lock

Keep a target near your sight until the box turns green. □ / X launches a homing missile. Missiles recharge; laser kills build a score chain.

03 / Stay slippery

L1 / Q or R1 / E barrel rolls and deflects shots. ○ / Shift boosts; L2 / Ctrl brakes. Both spend the engine meter, which recharges.

04 / Clear the sky

△ / B detonates a nova bomb. Three per sortie. Fly through gold rings for shields and bonus score. Cyan crystals upgrade your lasers.

05 / Bring them home

At the distress call, destroy the amber-marked pursuer before it escapes. Your wingmate rewards a rescue with a shield refill and a bomb.

06 / Crack the carrier

Destroy both shield pods, then the glowing reactor. Watch for spread fire. Options / Esc pauses. Earn three medals: rescue, ace (5,000), and intact (50% shields).

About this flight.

StarCoyote 64 — The Long Way Home is an original spiritual successor inspired by StarFox 64. All ships, environments, characters, and sounds are original procedural creations. No source game assets are included. Title chosen by a real random draw from 50 candidates.

Engine feedback

Liked most: Three.js geometry and scene graph made original spacecraft expressive. Got stuck: strict WebGPU initialization and an intermittent Rapier 0.19.3 collider-churn trap. Built ourselves: mission scripting, combat and targeting, controller UI, scoring, squadron events, and procedural audio. Hardest: keeping scrolling physics stable through destruction and reuse; a preallocated sensor pool resolved the reproduced failure. Would overhaul: physics lifecycle diagnostics. Would change: add an explicit strict WebGPU-only renderer option.

Related upstream report: Rapier collider-churn issue #363. This build retains the requested 0.19.3 version and leases sensors from a stable pool.
