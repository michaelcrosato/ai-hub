Solar Swift — The Meridian Run

An original arcade flight game inspired by StarFox 64. All craft, characters, environments, music and effects are procedurally authored for this demo. No Nintendo assets or characters are used. Original development: OpenAI coding agent.

Engine Feedback

Canvas offers direct control and a dependency-free artifact. The essential custom work was the flight/combat simulation, projection, mission director and controller state machine. No prebuilt scene systems were available; depth and combat tuning required implementation choices. The hardest part was combining readable targeting, depth and mission pacing. For production, I would overhaul the immediate scene drawing into a retained mesh graph. A standard depth-aware vector renderer would be the most useful engine addition.

Flight systems & accessibility

Progress and settings save locally when browser storage is available. No accounts or network access. Explorer mode reduces incoming damage; aim assist broadens laser targeting. Reduce motion disables camera shake and strong banking. Audio begins after an interaction. The mission has checkpoints and can always be retried. Attract mode is an invulnerable AI demonstration and loops automatically.
