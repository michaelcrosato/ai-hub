About the flight

THE EMBER RUN

Fly with Kite and Mako through the fractured canyons of Vesta. Hold fire to build a lock, release for a homing burst. Destroy the carrier's two shield pods before attacking its core. Passing gold rings restores shields; cyan cells restore bombs. Choose the left canyon for supplies or the right ridge for a harder interception. Your wingmates can clear a wave when called. A checkpoint begins at the carrier.

FLIGHT MANUAL

Left stick / WASD or arrows: fly · ✕ / Space: hold to fire and charge, release a lock shot · R1 / E: barrel roll, deflect hostile fire · L1 / Q: smart bomb · R2 / Shift: boost · L2 / Ctrl: brake · △ / R: wingmate assist · Options / Escape: pause. D-pad navigates menus; ✕ confirms and ○ returns. Roll and boost spend energy; energy recharges. Aim the reticle over a target for a charge lock. Gold rings repair 18 shield; every fifth kill earns a bomb. Wingmate assist recharges in 22 seconds.

ENGINE FEEDBACK

TSL made stepped lighting expressive. WebGPU needs explicit gating to avoid automatic fallback. Mission direction, flight controls, lock targeting and controller UI were custom built. Coordinating flight-space collisions and visuals was the hardest integration. I would overhaul implicit fallback and add a strict WebGPU-only initialization option.

PROMPT FEEDBACK · 404299fcd4

The clear scope and controller-first requirements helped. “Complete engine infrastructure” versus a single-file demo was ambiguous. Packaging a full mission with readable controls was hardest. The most valuable additional test would be a full PS5-controller mission on native WebGPU.
