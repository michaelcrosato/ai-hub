10:00AMOPENING TALKMake room for bad ideas.Maya Chen · Main stage
The first idea is rarely the best one. Maya shows how small experiments can help a team move past the fear of getting it wrong. Includes a short welcome and audience questions. Ends at 11:00 AM.
11:30AMTALK + Q&ABuild for the human part.Jordan Ellis · Main stage
Good tools begin with real people. Explore simple ways to listen, test assumptions, and make useful products. Bring one question about a problem you are working on. Ends at 12:15 PM.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might ask.

Who is OFFSCRIPT for?

Designers, builders, founders, students, and anyone who likes new ideas. You do not need a certain job title or level of experience.

What does my pass include?

Each sample pass includes all talks, both workshops, lunch, refreshments, and the end-of-day social. All Access adds reserved front-row seating and an event print.

Is this a real event or booking?

This is a working website demo for a fictional event. The speakers, venue, prices, and capacity are sample content. An RSVP is saved only in this browser. It does not reserve a real seat or send an email.

Can I change or delete my demo RSVP?

Use “Delete this demo RSVP” on your receipt, then submit a new entry. Use “Clear demo data” at the bottom of this page to remove all entries stored by this site on this device.
