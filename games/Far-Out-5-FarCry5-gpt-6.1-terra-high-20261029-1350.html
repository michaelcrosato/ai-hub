About / Credits

Far Out 5 is an original playable homage to the open-country action and unsettling rural antagonism of Far Cry 5. It is not affiliated with Ubisoft.

Source information: 2026-10-29 13:50. Source: Far Cry 5. Three.js r180 WebGPU; Rapier 0.19.3; Gamepad API; AI Hub session v1.3.0. Procedural cel-shaded low-poly art and animation. Synthesized sound effects; no music or voice. Online: AI Hub parallel-session reporting when hosted, no peer-to-peer world. Testing: lightweight browser and viewport checks. Environment: Codex CLI (headless), Linux, browser unknown. Model: OpenAI gpt-6.1-terra, reasoning effort high. Usage: unknown.

Testing feedback: Issue count and critical issue count: unknown. Most helpful: browser execution. Least helpful: unknown.

Engine feedback: WebGPU provides a modern 3D renderer. CDN/module and browser support required care. The procedural world, combat, and input systems were built for this demo. Integration was the hardest part. Better WebGPU deployment diagnostics would help.

Prompt feedback: Controller-first design and attract mode were useful. Hub multiplayer semantics were ambiguous. The hardest part was fitting open-country action into one file. A real PS5 controller and real iOS Safari test would be valuable.
