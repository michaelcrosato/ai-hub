There is no fixed up direction. Strafe while you fire. Shoot doors to open them. Fly into supplies and crew pods to collect them. Blue and red doors need matching keys. Watch for a loose ceiling panel. Hive pods keep breeding swarmers until you destroy them.

DOWN / SELENE EXTRACTION
An original six-axis mine combat game. One complete mission, made as a tribute to the feel of 1990s PC tunnel shooters.

Recover two keys. Rescue three crew members. Destroy the reactor, then find the exit before the timer ends. The route guide can help you through the mine; toggle it from the pause menu. East of the ore foundry, a sealed coolant sump holds the station's flight recorder, and something that breeds in the cold.

A checkpoint is saved when you take a key, find a secret or destroy the reactor. If your ship is lost you can retry from the last one, and Continue on the title screen picks up a mission you left.

The level, models, textures, music and sound effects are generated by this page. Rendered with three.js (WebGPU, or WebGL2 where WebGPU is missing) and Web Audio. Keyboard and mouse, gamepad and touch all work.

This is an independent fan-made work. It is not an official Descent game. Descent is a reference for the design, not the source of the assets.

Settings, best scores, best times and the last checkpoint stay in this browser when local storage is available.
