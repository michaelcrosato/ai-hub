BUILD. TEST. CHANGE.

Place one start tile. Join the connectors to make a closed lap, or use a finish tile for a point-to-point course. A road at 24 m must meet a road at 24 m. Use ramps to change height. The loop exit is offset to keep both road surfaces clear.

A drag is one undo step. Roads and terrain use separate layers. Erase only affects the selected layer. Use the Terrain tab for a 1, 3, or 5-cell brush and flood fill. Right-click erases. On touch screens, use PAN to move the view. In 3D, drag to turn the camera. Use the + and − buttons to zoom.

A crossroads keeps both roads on the ground. An overpass puts the arrow road 24 m above the side road. A test drive follows the connected route from the start. Unused roads are scenery. Leave at least two straight cells before a stunt, and brake before tight turns.
