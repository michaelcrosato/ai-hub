Vertical Slice Mission Briefing

Pilot the experimental Viper MK-IV Hydroplane through a continuous 4-phase river operation: (1) Rapids Gauntlet against currents & mines, (2) Emerald Lagoon swimmer rescue using your Verlet Tow-Cable, (3) The Great Cataract vertical waterfall jump ascent using retractable Aero-Wings, and (4) Caldera Boss Battle against Kraken-IX: The Iron Leviathan.

Declarative Level Expansion

New levels are added by pushing a JSON descriptor into LevelRegistry.levels[] defining splineControlPoints, zones, and hazardWaves. All water wakes, cliff walls, waterfall foam, and minimap paths auto-generate from the spline.

Architecture Highlights

• IsoProjection: 30° isometric projection with true vertical Z-height, dynamic water surface reflections, and drop shadows.
• VerletTowSystem: Multi-node spring constraints allowing up to 3 swimmers or disarmed mines to trail behind the boat.
• WiseWave Audio: Zero-sample procedural WebAudio synthesizer generating David Wise-inspired slap-bass & lead arpeggios plus dynamic RPM engine audio.
