Kestrel Mk. IV / Pilot handbook

Trust your wings.

Fly with the left stick. Your guns follow your flight line. Everything else is a well-timed button.

Left stick WASD / arrows

Fly & aim

Steer through the reticle. Keep targets near the center of your flight line.

R2 Space / left click

Pulse lasers

Hold to fire. Chain kills quickly to build your score multiplier.

L2 Ctrl / right click

Lock & release

Hold until the orange lock completes. Release to launch a seeking missile.

L1 / R1 Q / E

Barrel roll

Tap either shoulder to roll and reflect incoming plasma. Briefly shields your hull.

✕ / □ Shift / Alt

Boost / brake

Change your pace and dodge. Share a regenerating engine energy supply.

○ / △ X / F

Nova / wing support

Nova clears nearby threats. Call your wingmates for an assist. R3 / R flips in the arena.
