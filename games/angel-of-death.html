The aircraft is always in a left orbit. The ground view turns as you circle. Gun travel is limited. An amber sight marks an aim limit; fire is blocked outside the limit. The pilot responds after a short delay. The turn does not stop the orbit.

Shells take time to arrive. Aim ahead of moving targets. The 105 mm has a large blast area. Do not fire near green markers or the protected clinic. All weapons reload automatically. Reserve ammunition is unlimited.

Touch: drag on the view to aim. Hold FIRE to shoot. Use the weapon, zoom, and map buttons. Tap ADJUST to request an aircraft turn. Hold your aim at a limit for an automatic turn request.
