A FEW GOOD MOVES

Find your rhythm.

One. Two. Three!

Run, then jump. Press jump again as you land. Do this twice to get the high third jump and a full flip. Keep moving. Hold jump for more height.

Dive into a slide.

Press dive on the ground or in the air. Land on your belly, then slide forward. Jump to get back on your feet.

Make an impact.

Press stomp in the air. Pause, tuck, then drop fast. The impact breaks crates and presses orange switches.

Touch Left stick to move. Right side to look, pinch to zoom. Three large action buttons; the camera button centres the view.

Keyboard WASD / arrows to move. Space to jump. J / Shift to dive. K to stomp. Drag the mouse or Q / E to orbit, wheel to zoom. C to centre. M for map. Escape to pause. F9 for graphics.

Gamepad Left stick to move. Right stick to look. A to jump, X or RT to dive, B or LT to stomp. Y to centre, LB / RB to zoom. Back for the map, Start to pause.
