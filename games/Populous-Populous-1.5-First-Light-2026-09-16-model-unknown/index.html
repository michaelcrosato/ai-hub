A WORLD IN YOUR HANDS

Small people.
Great possibilities.

Shape the land. Grow your followers. Defeat the rival god.

01

Make flat land

Select Level, then tap near your homes. Followers build and upgrade their own homes. More flat land supports larger settlements.

02

Guide, do not command

Settle makes followers seek a home. Gather sends them to their leader and your magnet. Fight sends them towards the enemy.

03

Use your power

Followers give you mana. Swamps, earthquakes, volcanoes, and floods can harm both sides. Use a knight to attack. Armageddon starts the final battle.

04

Win the world

Remove all rival followers. You lose when none of your followers remain. Low land floods. Change the land to clear a swamp, ash or damaged ground.

Land powers cost twice as much beyond your followers’ reach. The Long Dawn is ten worlds in a row, each with a stronger rival; custom worlds let you choose the land, the rival and the seed. Worlds save in this browser. It is not an official Populous release.
