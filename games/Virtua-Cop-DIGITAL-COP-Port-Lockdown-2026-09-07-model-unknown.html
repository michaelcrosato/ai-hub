OFFICER FIELD GUIDE

AIM. FIRE. RELOAD.

The camera moves for you. Stop armed suspects and protect the dock workers.

01 / READ THE THREAT

Point and click, or tap a suspect, to fire. A shrinking ring turns green → yellow → red. Fire before it closes.

02 / COUNT YOUR ROUNDS

Six shots. Press R, Space, or the right mouse button to reload. On a phone, tap RELOAD. Reload before you run dry.

03 / MAKE THE RIGHT CALL

Do not shoot workers with raised hands. Hit a suspect’s gun hand for a disarm bonus. Shoot supply boxes for health or an AUTO-9. Hold to fire the AUTO-9. It has one 12-round magazine.

04 / GRENADIERS

Aboard the MORNING STAR, grenadiers lob explosives. Shoot a grenade out of the air, or hit it in his hand and he fumbles it. The blast takes out anyone close by.
