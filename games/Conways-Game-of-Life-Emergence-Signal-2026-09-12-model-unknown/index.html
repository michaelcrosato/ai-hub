THE FIELD GUIDE

Life follows three rules.

01

Birth

A dead cell with exactly three live neighbors becomes alive.

02

Survival

A live cell with two or three live neighbors stays alive.

03

Death

All other live cells die. All other dead cells stay dead.

All cells update at the same time. Each cell has eight neighbors. Height, glow, and color are visual only. “Wrap edges” joins opposite sides of the field.

Move through the field

Desktop: Drag to orbit. Use the wheel to zoom. Right-drag to pan.

Touch: Use one finger to orbit. Pinch to zoom. In Top view, one finger pans.

Gamepad: The left stick moves a cursor on the field, A uses the tool, the right stick orbits, LB and RB zoom. Start opens the menu.

Select Draw, Erase, or Stamp to edit. Editing pauses the simulation. Right-drag and pinch still move the camera.

Controls for your device

This is a finite field. A pattern can collide with the boundary or with its own wrapped output. That can change its long-term behavior.
