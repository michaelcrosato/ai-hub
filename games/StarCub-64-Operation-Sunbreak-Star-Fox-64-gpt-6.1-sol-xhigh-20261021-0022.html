Hold. Lock. Let go. Lasers fire while charging. After 0.8 seconds, release a homing plasma shot. Amber brackets show your lock. Blue capsules add twin then hyper lasers.

Roll through danger. A barrel roll reflects red projectiles and prevents collision damage. Boost and brake share a regenerating energy bank. Bombs clear projectiles and damage everything ahead.

Fly through gold rings for shield repair; every third ring also adds a bomb. At the canyon fork, fly left for the ridge or right for the harbor. Protect Pip during the ambush. The carrier's exposed amber parts are vulnerable; cyan armor is invulnerable.

Flight log / original production

Built for the big sky.

StarCub 64 — Operation Sunbreak. Original starfighters, characters, world, music and effects created for this demo. Inspired by the pace, legibility and low-poly charm of StarFox 64. No Nintendo assets are included.
