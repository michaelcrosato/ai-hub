FIELD MANUAL · CONTROLS & TACTICS
Select & move

Tap a blue soldier or a card. Then tap the ground or a flag. Soldiers fire on their own. Tap a red unit to focus fire.

Win the battle

Hold more flags to drain the red score. Stay near a flag to capture it. More friendly soldiers capture faster. Enemy soldiers stop a capture.

Use cover

Sandbags block fire from one side. Go around them to flank. Houses block shots too. Machine guns pin soldiers down. Smoke blocks fire. Grenades break cover.

Keep them standing

Patch heals and revives nearby soldiers. Aid drops heal and revive in a marked area. Retreat to base for free healing. Lost soldiers return after a short delay.

Touch controls

Tap to select or order. Drag the map with one finger. Pinch to zoom. Tap the small map to move the camera. Landscape gives you a wider view.

Mouse & keys

Left-click to select or order. Drag to box-select. Shift adds to the selection. Right-click also gives orders. Right-drag or WASD moves the camera. Wheel or +/− zooms, V shows the whole map. 1–6 selects a unit, C cycles soldiers, Q selects all. G/F/E/T/R gives orders. Space or P pauses. Esc cancels an order or pauses. Enter deploys. H opens this manual, M mutes, F9 opens graphics settings.

Controller

Left stick moves the cursor, A selects or orders. X selects all, LT/RT picks a soldier. D-pad: ↑ grenade, ← smoke, → aid drop, ↓ tin tank. Y retreats, B cancels an order. Right stick pans, LB/RB zoom, right-stick click shows the whole map. Push the cursor to a screen edge to scroll. Start pauses, View opens this manual. In menus the D-pad jumps between buttons.

Armor

Tanks shrug off rifle fire. Bazookas and grenades hurt them most. Your Tin Tank costs 90 supplies and can be called again after it is lost. Tanks fight, but cannot capture flags.

Blue: Pocket Patrol. Red: Red Rascals. All units are toys. The battle ends at zero score or after six minutes. At the time limit, the higher score wins. A tie goes to sudden death. This is an original game inspired by small-squad action and cover-based strategy. It has no link to the owners of its inspirations.
