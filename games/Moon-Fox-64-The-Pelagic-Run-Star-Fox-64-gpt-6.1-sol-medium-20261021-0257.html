Hold fire while your reticle covers a target. When the bracket turns orange, release for a homing blast. Barrel rolls reflect incoming shots. Follow gold rings for repairs; cyan diamonds upgrade twin lasers. Flying left or right chooses your canyon route.

Right stick also fine-tunes aim. In Settings, both flight axes can be inverted. Controller mappings follow the standard Gamepad API layout. Keyboard mouse clicks work in menus; flight uses the same stick-style controls.

Moon Fox 64

An original spiritual successor to StarFox 64. Inspired by its expressive rail-flight combat and squad camaraderie. All models, scenery, dialogue and sound here are original procedural work. No Nintendo assets are used.
