FIELD MANUAL

One base. Four fronts.

Keep the command core alive through 12 waves. Enemies cross the full map. They attack troops, break walls, and reach the core through gaps.

BUILD & DEFEND

Use the bottom bar or keys 1–8. Place extractors on amber deposits. Generators supply power. Field stations extend your build area and heal nearby troops.

Select a building. Use U to upgrade or H to repair. Select a barracks and use T to train four troops for 180 metal.

A power shortage slows weapons, training, and extractors. Each player has a free support strike every 55 seconds.

CONTROL YOUR TROOPS

Click or drag to select. Hold Shift to add troops. Right click to move or attack an enemy. Hold Shift and right click to attack while you move.

Tab selects your troops. Ctrl + 1–4 saves a control group. Alt + 1–4 selects that group. F aims a support strike.

WASD moves the camera. Q / E rotates. Scroll to zoom. Middle-drag to pan. M shows the map. Space returns to the core.

CO-OP: Metal, power, buildings, and the core are shared. Each commander controls their own troops. Empty player slots use guard squads. These guards fight, but do not build or spend metal.
