Not a race to the next hit.

The small gold bar below health is your weapon's power. Holding attack waits for a full swing. Tapping early trades damage for immediacy.

The same magic, everywhere.

Ember opens dry husks. Tide carries water and soaks enemies. Ember on a soaked foe creates steam. Bloom heals, revives nearby companions, and wakes seedbeds.

Read the ground.

Gold outlines warn before attacks land. Step out of solid marks; dodge through an expanding ring. Restored chimes heal freely and save checkpoints.

Local two-player: F2 joins a second hero. P2 uses arrow keys, I to strike, O to cast, and right Shift to dodge. P1 handles interactions and menus.

Standard gamepad: left stick moves, south button dodges, west attacks, east casts, north opens the ring. Shoulders switch hero; Start pauses. A second controller can join with its south button.
