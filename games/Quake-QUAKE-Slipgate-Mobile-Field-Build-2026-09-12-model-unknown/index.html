DRIVER CHECK

Use the GPU driver supplied through Samsung system updates. Update your browser. Do not install a random “gaming driver.” Check chrome://gpu for browser GPU status. WebGPU needs HTTPS or localhost.

Driver versions and GPU temperature are not exposed by this app. This build has not been measured on a physical S26.

The render test uses a fixed camera route through the Cinder Vault. It is not a full gameplay benchmark.

SURVIVE THE VAULT

MOVE. AIM. FIRE.

TOUCH

Put your left thumb down anywhere on the left side to move; push the stick to the edge to run. Drag on the right side to aim. Hold Fire to shoot. Jump, Dash and Swap sit next to it. II pauses.

KEYBOARD + MOUSE

WASD or the arrows move and the mouse aims (click the view to capture it). Click or J fires. Space jumps. Shift runs. Q or right click dashes. 1–3 or the wheel change weapons. Esc or P pauses. F9 opens graphics settings.

GAMEPAD

Left stick moves, right stick aims, click the left stick to run. RT or X fires, A jumps, B or LT dashes, LB / RB or Y change weapons. Start pauses. In menus, the D-pad moves, A picks and B goes back.

THE MISSION

Clear every wave. Stay out of the lava. Find health, armor, and ammo. Then move to the blue gate. Each arena hides one secret. The Cinder Vault has three waves; the Ash Cloister has four, and wraiths that fly above the lava moat.

YOUR QUAKE DATA

Load a local pak0.pak file to explore Quake BSP29 maps with original textures. The map explorer does not run QuakeC. Doors, lifts, original monsters, and scripted level progress are not implemented. Press N, the gamepad View button or the up/down button for free flight. Jump moves up. Run or Dash moves down.

This is an independent Quake-inspired game, not a complete port. Original game data is not included or uploaded.

BRING YOUR OWN SLIPGATE

LOCAL QUAKE DATA.

Choose a PAK file from your Quake installation. Files stay on this device. They are not sent to a server.

Quake assets have separate license terms. Do not include them in a public site without permission.

Map geometry, palette textures, basic baked lighting, and world collision are supported. Game scripts and animated Quake models are not supported.
