Flight, mission authoring, combat, audio, menus and persistence were custom built. Coordinating physics and scrolling flight space was hardest. Field notes

The left fork opens the dangerous refinery shortcut: more enemies, more score. The right fork follows the rescue corridor: fly through rings to repair your ship and keep your wingmate alive. At the carrier, break both orange engine pods before the pink core becomes vulnerable. Your charge shot tracks locked targets. Nova bombs clear hostile fire. Wingmate support recharges in 18 seconds.

Menus: D-pad / arrows, ✕ / Enter to select, ○ / Escape to return. In this archive use the right stick or Page Up / Page Down to scroll. No touch flight controls are provided. Internet is required for CDN libraries and optional fonts. Serve over localhost or HTTPS for WebGPU.
