Mission notes

KESTREL — You are Pip, a courier turned interceptor pilot. Rook handles reconnaissance; Mica runs systems. Your objective is to stop the Aster Dominion’s solar harvester before it fractures the Glass Meridian. The refinery route gives more supplies; the storm route offers more score. Both lead to the Citadel. Destroy its two emitters, then its core.
