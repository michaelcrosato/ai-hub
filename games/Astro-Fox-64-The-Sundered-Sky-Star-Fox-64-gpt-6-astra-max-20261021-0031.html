ASTRO FOX 64 / THE SUNDERED SKY
An original arcade flight adventure inspired by StarFox 64. All aircraft, characters, scenery, music, and effects are original procedural work. No source-game assets are included. Not affiliated with Nintendo.

Engine feedback

Most liked: Three.js scene graphs and reusable geometry made a cohesive procedural world straightforward. Rapier supplied the sensor and ray-query tools used by flight combat.

Got stuck or guessed wrong: Three.js r180 automatically offers WebGL fallback; this project explicitly disables it. Sustained combat also exposed a collider-churn crash in the required Rapier version. Reusing physics bodies resolved it. See Rapier issue 363. The headless Chromium compositor required direct canvas readback for reliable screenshots.

Most important custom system: the mission director, integrating rail flight, all-range combat, rescue objectives, branching routes, checkpoints, and a multi-stage boss.

Hardest part: physics-object lifecycle and keeping aiming, camera motion, controller input, and projected HUD markers consistent across flight modes.

What to overhaul: implicit renderer fallback. One change: a public, documented strict-WebGPU initialization option with clear capability errors.
