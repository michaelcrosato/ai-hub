Mouse: Move to steer and aim. Hold the left button to fire. Press the right button for a pulse.

Touch: Drag in the play area to steer. Hold FIRE with your other thumb. Use MAP and II at the top.

Gamepad: Left stick to steer. A to fire, B for a pulse, X to boost, Y for the map. Start to pause.

02 / THE WAY HOME

1. Aster Reach. Fly through four cyan pilot signals. Only left and right alignment matters. A missed pilot comes around again.

2. Needle Run. Collect each cyan key before its barrier. Fly through three open barriers.

3. The Nightjar. Destroy both bright shield nodes. Then attack the central core.

4. Solace. Use the scanner to jump to the safe star. Bring everyone home.

Haven repairs your hull and restores fuel and pulses. Fly into its green docking bay. The raid fleet is an optional score mission.

Keep both diamonds together during warp to save fuel. Let the photon gun cool when it gets hot. A nova pulse clears incoming shots. Boost uses extra fuel.
