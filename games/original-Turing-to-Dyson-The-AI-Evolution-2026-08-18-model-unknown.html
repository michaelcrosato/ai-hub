How to play

Build → research → train → evaluate → deploy → reinvest

  1. Build. Choose a facility and place it on the 11 × 11 grid. Orthogonally adjacent facilities share one service bus, and every bus needs a power source. Keep cooling within two tiles of each compute rack. Disconnected facilities show dimmed.
  2. Research. Labs and evidence earn research points. Each era has four milestones; its training method (Perceptron, Backpropagation, Transformer, Automated science, Causal networking) unlocks model training.
  3. Train. Pick capacity, learning rate and regularization, then watch training and validation loss diverge. Keep the best checkpoint before a model overfits, and curate data when quality is low.
  4. Deploy. Models scoring 40%+ can be released. Deployments earn revenue but reserve 30% of compute for inference.
  5. Advance. To enter the next era: all four milestones, a deployed model scoring 60%+, trust of 60+, cool and fully powered infrastructure, a cash reserve, and the era's public review.
