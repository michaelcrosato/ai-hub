Engine feedback

Three.js geometry composition and its scene graph were the strongest tools for the procedural world. I got stuck on a Rapier 0.19.3 broad-phase crash during collider cleanup; a persistent terrain collider prevents the problematic single-leaf state, and body removals are deferred to the physics boundary. The most important custom system is the flight and mission director, including AI, targeting, checkpoints, input and menus. Physics lifecycle integration was the hardest part. I would overhaul automatic WebGL fallback and add a first-class strict-WebGPU option. Related upstream BVH fix.

Prompt feedback · 404299fcd4

Prompt version 404299fcd4. The controller-first vertical slice and attract mode provided a strong target. The boundary between a demo and infrastructure for an entire campaign was broad. The hardest task was pacing every mechanic into a readable, self-contained mission while resolving the pinned physics engine edge case. The additional test I would choose is a full sortie on physical PS5 hardware with an independent player.

Runtime dependencies download from jsDelivr. Internet access and a WebGPU-capable browser on a secure origin (HTTPS or localhost) are required. No WebGL fallback. Audio begins after an input gesture.
