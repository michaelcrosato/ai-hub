A shape, one circle at a time.

Each circle is a Fourier term. Its size and turn rate come from the shape. The circles join end to end. Their last point draws the path.

z(t) = c₀ + Σ cₖ e2πikt

Use fewer terms for a simple path. Add terms for finer details. Change the speed to watch the circles work. Drag the progress control to check any part of the loop.

In Draw your own, draw one line. The ends join to make a loop. Select Animate drawing to start. All drawing and math stay in this page.

The page samples 512 equally spaced points along your path. A discrete Fourier transform finds the circles. It puts larger terms first. The fixed centre term, c₀, is not part of the term count. The save button exports the full reconstructed path as an SVG file.
