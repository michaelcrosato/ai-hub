How to play

Hold fire. Collect stars for points, hearts for repairs, and P for stronger shots. Chain defeats for a score multiplier. Bombs clear bullets and hurt enemies; dashing gives brief invulnerability. Beat both bosses to finish the demo. In a hub, everyone flies separately on the same seeded course; highest score wins.

Engine feedback

Three.js makes playful 3D approachable. Stuck or guessed wrong: unknown. The encounter and input state machines were built for this game. Coordinating WebGPU, physics and controllers in a portable file was hardest. A clearer WebGPU-only initialization and a first-class no-fallback option would help.

Prompt feedback

The controller-first arcade and attract-mode constraints were inspiring. The exact multiplayer format was unspecified. A complete portable vertical slice was the hardest part. An additional real PS5 controller and real iOS Safari test would be valuable.
