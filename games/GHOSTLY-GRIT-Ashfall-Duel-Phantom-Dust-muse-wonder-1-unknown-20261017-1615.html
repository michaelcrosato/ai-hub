HOW TO PLAY

You are a psychic duellist in ruined Ashfall City. Reduce rival Vesk to 0 HP before the 3:00 timer ends — or hold more HP when it does.

THE AURA LOOP (the heart of the duel)

  • Skills cost Aura (blue pips). Aura slowly refills up to your cap.
  • Stand at a glowing Aura Pillar when its orb appears to capture it: +1 max Aura and a full refill. Pillars cycle — fight over them.
  • Your hand holds 4 skills. Attacks wear out after a few uses — the next skill is drawn from your 30-card Arsenal. Watch the deck count.

CONTROLS — PS5 DUALSENSE (PRIMARY)

  • Left stick move · Right stick camera · L1 lock-on · R1 jump / hover · R2 dash (briefly untouchable)
  • Skills: □ △ ✕ ○ fire the matching hand slot · OPTIONS pause

CONTROLS — KEYBOARD

  • WASD move · Arrow keys / mouse camera · Q / Tab lock-on · Space jump · Shift dash
  • Skills: 1 2 3 4 (also J K L U) · P / Esc pause · Menus: arrows + Enter, back Esc

READING THE FIGHT

  • Lock on to track Vesk through ruins; break line of sight behind rubble to dodge.
  • Aegis Veil blocks all damage for 3s. Blink Step teleports you forward. Shock Nova punishes anyone crowding you.
  • Meteors telegraph a ring — step out of it. Mines glow amber on the ground.

GHOSTLY GRIT is an original spiritual successor to the source below. No source code, art, music or assets were used; every model is built in code, every sound is synthesised.

SOURCE INFORMATION

Initial Date: 2026-10-17
SOURCE: Phantom Dust (2004, Xbox) — Microsoft Game Studios Japan, dir. Yukio Futatsugi. Inspiration only.
Tech/Engine: Three.js 0.180.0 (pinned CDN, ES module import map), WebGL2, Web Audio API, vanilla JavaScript/CSS. Single HTML file, no build step.
Environment: Muse Code CLI, Linux (WSL2), Chrome/Edge browser, PS5 DualSense + keyboard.
Model and Effort Level: Meta — muse-wonder-1.0 (Muse model family), reasoning effort unknown.

PROMPT REFLECTION

What I liked: the source's deck + aura loop is a distinctive brief, and the attract-mode / controller-first constraints force a real arcade loop. What was confusing: fidelity vs. modernisation is open-ended and "feature-complete" must be scoped to one level. Hardest: deck/hand skills, aura economy, lock-on combat, psychic AI, AI-vs-AI attract play and gamepad-only menus in one file.
