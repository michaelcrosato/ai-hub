Mango Bandit

WUMPA WILDS

A cel-shaded jungle platformer inspired by Crash Bandicoot (PS1). Spin, bounce, belly-flop and slide through Crate Canyon — smash every crate, grab the Crystal, and escape through the portal!

Controller: Left Stick move • ✕ Jump • □ Spin • ○ Slide • △ Belly-Flop • Options Pause
Keyboard: WASD/Arrows • Space Jump • J Spin • K Slide • L Flop • Shift Sprint • P/Esc Pause

⚠ Controller or keyboard required — no touch controls. Settings still available above.

Stay idle 10s for Attract Mode demo…

How to Play

Move WASD / Arrows / Left Stick   Jump Space / ✕ (press again mid-air = double jump)

Spin J / □ — destroys crates & enemies, deflects danger. Slide K / ○ — slide under, slide-jump for distance.

Belly-Flop L / △ in air — smashes iron crates & ground pounds. Sprint Shift / R2.

📦 Wood crate: spin/jump on it. ? crate: bonus Wumpa. 🧨 TNT: touch starts 3-2-1… run! 💚 Nitro: don't touch — spin nearby crates only, it explodes on contact. ⬆ Iron bounce crate: trampoline. C Checkpoint crate. 🪶 Mask crate: shield (3 = invincible rainbow run!).

Collect 100 🥭 = extra life. Break ALL crates for the 💎 Gem. Grab the 🔮 Crystal halfway, then reach the swirling portal. Falling or losing all masks costs a life — checkpoints save you.

Enemies: crabs & turtles patrol — spin or stomp them. Spiky plants: spin only, never stomp.
