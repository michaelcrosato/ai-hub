AS–07 / PILOT REFERENCE

FIELD MANUAL

MOVEMENT

A D or ← → Move the suit.

SPACE Jump. Hold in the air to hover.

SHIFT Boost. This uses thrust fuel.

Fuel fills again when you do not use thrust.

WEAPONS & DEFENSE

J / Z Fire. W S / ↑ ↓ Aim.

C Hold to lock your aim.

L / X Hold the front shield. Release to fire.

Q Switch weapon. R Reload.

V Strike at close range.

MISSION INTELLIGENCE

Destroy the bulkhead at the end of the hangar.

Cross the broken decks. Green beacons save progress.

Destroy the Warden. Hit its core when it glows blue.

Reach the exit before the reactor fails.

OTHER CONTROLS

Mouse: aim and left-click to fire. Right-click to shield.

Gamepad: left stick moves (push up or down to aim), right stick aims. A jump · X / RT fire · B boost · LT shield · RB strike · Y weapon · LB lock aim · L3 reload · Start pause.

Touch: the stick moves and aims. JUMP, FIRE, BOOST, SHIELD and STRIKE on the right; SWAP, RELOAD and II (pause) along the top.

ESC Pause. M Sound. F Full screen. F9 Graphics.

Collect P cells to raise cannon damage. Collect green cells to repair armor. Break supply crates for rockets. Red floor marks warn of a strike. Move before it lands.
