Flight manual // Aster Wing

Fly fast. Aim true.

Your ship flies a fixed corridor. Steer through enemy fire, line up the reticle, and defend your wingmates through three linked sorties.

Steer & aimLeft stick / WASD steers. Right stick / mouse aims. Aim follows your ship when idle.
Laser & lockR2 / Space fires. Hold R1 / F to charge a homing burst; release when a target locks.
Boost & brakeL2 / Shift boosts through danger. L1 / C brakes and builds a lock faster.
Roll & bomb○ / Q reflects shots during a barrel roll. □ / E detonates a screen-clearing bomb.
Pickups & ringsFly through supply diamonds for repairs and bombs. Fly through gold rings for score.
Squad & routesKeep your squad alive for support fire. Pick a storm or wreckage route after the first boss.

About & credits

Astrotail Assault is an original, self-contained rail shooter inspired by the fast formations, expressive squad banter, route choices, defensive rolls, and giant bosses of a Nintendo 64 classic. All ships, worlds, names, dialogue, art, and sound are newly made for this demo.
