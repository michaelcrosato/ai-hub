Hunt, then break the wards. Rescue all three captives and silence three ward stones. Living guards within 13 metres protect their stone. The eastern relic grants a midair dash. Headshots deal double damage. The Sunfang bow draws while held; the Scatterthorn clears packs; the Burrower seeks a marked enemy and erupts after a delay. L2 aim assists with a controller.

Swim in the western channel to find the hidden cache. Use the plaza shrine to heal and record a checkpoint. After death, your mission progress remains. The Rootheart is vulnerable between its spore volleys; dash through its shockwave.

Physical controllers and separate-device WAN/NAT conditions: unknown.

Environment: Codex CLI (headless); Linux; target browser unknown. Validation browser: Google Chrome 154.0.8037.57, headed through Xvfb with an explicit Vulkan SwiftShader WebGPU adapter.

Testing Feedback

Issues caught: 23 corrected issue categories across testing and integration review, documented in QA.md.

Critical / game-breaking: Three, all fixed: corrupted packaged module, broken gate reset, and a missing-body dereference on reinforcements.

Most helpful test: The upstream dedicated-server harness exposed a brief-controller-input sampling defect; a queued and deduplicated input edge now survives between rendered frames.

Least helpful test: Syntax-only checks; they missed HTML replacement corruption and could not establish rendered pixels or controller behavior.

Engine Feedback

Liked most: Three.js node materials provide direct control of coherent cel shading; Rapier's character controller handles stairs and swimming-channel collision reliably.

Stuck or guessed wrong: The default Linux headless adapter produced blank output even for one cube. Explicit Vulkan SwiftShader adapter selection made actual WebGPU pixels testable.

Most important custom system: The mission/combat state machine, controller focus and edge capture, and host-authoritative co-op snapshots/events.

Hardest part: Combining responsive local movement, shared mission/enemy authority, input timing and browser graphics without relying on a prebuilt game framework.

Remove or overhaul: Implicit renderer backend fallback is unsuitable for an explicit WebGPU-only contract; the game checks the adapter and actual backend.

One engine change: A maintained first-person Rapier character and network-snapshot reference integrated with Three.js would reduce integration work.

Prompt Feedback

Liked most: Concrete deployment, controller, attract-mode, audio and multiplayer requirements alongside creative freedom.

Confusing: Feature-complete has no fixed content boundary. This deliverable defines one complete expedition with every advertised mechanic, a final encounter, a finish and checkpoint recovery.

Hardest task: Fitting exploration/combat, procedural art/audio, controller UX and two multiplayer environments into one portable HTML file.

One additional test: Four players on separate devices and networks, each using a physical DualSense controller, through a complete live expedition.
