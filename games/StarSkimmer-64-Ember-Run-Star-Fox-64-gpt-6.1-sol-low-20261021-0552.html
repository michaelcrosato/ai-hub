Fly through gold rings to repair shields. Cyan cores upgrade your lasers. At the canyon fork, steer left or right to choose your route. Rescue your wingmate for support fire. Break both boss pylons, then strike its core.

OPTIONS / ESC pauses. D-pad navigates menus. ✕ confirms, ○ returns. All controls can be practiced in the sortie. Hold fire for rapid lasers; lock builds automatically while aiming.
