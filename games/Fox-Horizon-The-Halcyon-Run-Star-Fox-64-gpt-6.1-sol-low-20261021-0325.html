Engine Feedback

Three.js makes procedural scene composition expressive. Explicitly disabling its implicit WebGL fallback required extra care. Flight feel, targeting, mission scripting and controller UI were built here. Coordinating targeting in camera space was the hardest part. A strict WebGPU option would improve the engine; implicit fallback is the feature I would overhaul.
