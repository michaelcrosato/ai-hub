How to Play

Goal: Collect 5 Melody Notes ♪ before the morning of Day 8. Earn notes by farming, watering, harvesting, shipping and befriending the valley.

One-button farming

Walk up to anything and press the action button. On a plot it does the right thing in order: Till → Plant → Water → Harvest. Watered crops grow overnight. Sleep at your farmhouse door to start a new day.

Seeds & money

Cycle seeds with Q / △. Buy more at Poppy's stall (north-west). Put harvests in the Shipping Bin by the barn to turn them into Gold.
