Stillwater focus

Focus slows danger for a short time. Aim at a guard and press X during focus to fire a shot that disarms them.

Crouch behind crates to block fire. Tall grass reduces detection. Watch the guards and the witness.

On a touch screen, drag on the left to move. Drag farther to run or gallop. Tap Ride to call Ash. Fire during focus to disarm.

With a gamepad, use the left stick to move, the right stick to aim, RT to fire, A to use, B to ride, X to reload, and LB for focus.

There is more than one way through. Talk at the main gate, find the creek route, or take the yard by force. You can change your plan at any time.
