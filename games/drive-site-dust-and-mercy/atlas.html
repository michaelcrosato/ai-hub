How the scale works

All world positions use metres. One metre equals 16 engine units. Zoom changes the view only.

The area is the horizontal map area. Height data shapes the map and travel cost. The ride uses a flat ground plane with depth-sorted art.

Route surveys follow the drawn line and ignore prop collisions. Free rides include collisions. Roads, rivers, plants, rocks, and places come from the seed. Places have structures. Story missions are part of the separate Bitter Creek chapter.
