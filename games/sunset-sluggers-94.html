THE SCOUTING REPORT

LET’S PLAY BALL.

You play as the Sunset Comets. Score more runs than the Bay City Bolts. Three outs switch sides.

01 / AT BAT

Click or drag on the field, or use the arrow keys to aim. On touch screens, tap or drag.

Press SPACE or SWING as the ball reaches the gold target. Press X for a stronger, harder-to-time swing.

The outer ring closes as the ball comes near. Swing when the rings meet.

02 / ON THE MOUND

Click, drag, or use the arrow keys to aim. Choose a pitch with 1 2 3.

Hold SPACE or PITCH. Release in the green band for your best pitch.

Mix your pitches. Balls outside the box can draw a swing, but four balls give a walk.

03 / IN THE FIELD

Your fielders chase the ball. Use the arrow keys to move the marked fielder. Press SPACE near the ball to dive.

Throws and base running are automatic.

04 / EXTRA BASES

Tied games go to extra innings with a runner on second base.

In the derby, you get 10 pitches. Only home runs score: 100 points plus the distance in feet.

ESC Pause · M Sound · F Full screen

Early swings go left. Late swings go right. A foul cannot be the third strike. Aim carefully and watch the ball.
