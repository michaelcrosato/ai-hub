HOW TO PLAY

🚀 Fly: Left stick / WASD / arrows. You auto-run forward — own the sky lane (left/right) and altitude (up/down).

🔫 Shoot: Hold ✕ or R2 (Space / J). Dash: ○ or L1 (Shift / K) — brief invincible burst, 1.2s cooldown. Crashing costs 1 ♥. Grab ★ stars for +500 and ♥ hearts (rare) to heal.

👾 Stage: Survive the run to 100%, then beat VORGATH. Boss dives and sprays shots — keep moving, shoot the glowing core. Clear = WIN. Lose all ♥ = game over. Near-misses and combos raise your score multiplier (up to ×8).

🏆 Scoring: Drone 100 · Turret 150 · Weaver 200 · Tree/pillar bust 50 · Star 500.
