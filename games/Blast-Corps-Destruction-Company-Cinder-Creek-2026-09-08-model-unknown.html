IRONCLAD: Hit wood and brick with the front blade. Use the special for more power.

SIDEWINDER: Build speed. Hold the special and steer. Slide the heavy rear into concrete.

PILEDRIVER: Press the special to jump. Land near structures to crush them.

Drive over green beacons. Use the side bridge to cross the creek. Vehicle recovery is automatic if you enter water.

GAMEPAD: Left stick or D-pad steers. RT drives. LT brakes and reverses. A: special. B: use or exit. LB / RB or X: switch crew. Y or a right-stick flick: turn the camera. Right stick up and down: zoom. Click the right stick: carrier view. Back: map. Start: pause.

TOUCH: The stick drives and steers. SPECIAL, USE and CREW sit on the right. Hold VIEW to watch the carrier. MAP opens the site map. Pinch to zoom. C VIEW and PAUSE are at the top.
