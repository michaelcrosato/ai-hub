About / credits / build notes

A LITTLE TROUBLE.
A LOT OF SKY.

Star Skipper 64 is an original spiritual successor to Starfox 64, built for a controller and a big screen. All ships, characters, environments, music, and sound effects are original procedural work. No source-game assets are used.
