About & credits

An original, compact fantasy adventure inspired by Avowed. Explore the Hollow, speak to its keeper, and decide the fate of its wounded heart.

Source: Avowed · 2026-10-29 11:04 · Three.js r180 WebGPU, Rapier 0.19.3, Gamepad API · Cel-shaded art · Procedural animation · Synthesized sound effects; no music or voice · AI Hub parallel sessions · Testing: lightweight viewport and browser checks · Codex CLI (headless), Linux, browser unknown · OpenAI gpt-6.1-terra, reasoning ultra · Usage: unknown.

Testing feedback: Two issues caught: missing hub script (fixed) and WebGPU device loss during software-rendered gameplay (critical to verification, unresolved). Most helpful: Chromium gameplay smoke test. Least helpful: initial viewport run against a misconfigured server. Engine feedback: WebGPU offers expressive rendering; compatibility and setup are challenging. Gameplay and interaction were built directly. Better diagnostics would help. Prompt feedback: Controller-first design was useful; the breadth of a complete game was challenging. A real controller and device test would be valuable.
