Fly through gold rings to repair your shield. Cyan capsules upgrade lasers. Hold a target inside your sights to lock a charged missile; its blast catches nearby enemies. Rolling reflects incoming fire and costs engine energy. Boost and brake share that energy.

At the fork, steer high for the open sky or low for the flooded ruins. Both routes lead to the carrier. Destroy its two shield generators, then keep moving while you attack the exposed core. The mission autosaves a retry checkpoint at each sector.

About the flight

Cosmic Coyote 64 is an original, procedurally built arcade flight demo. Inspiration: StarFox 64. No source game assets, characters, or audio are included. Title selected from 50 candidates using Node.js crypto.randomInt.

Engine feedback

Three.js makes procedural mesh composition and cinematic lighting expressive. Its WebGPU renderer defaults to a WebGL fallback, which had to be explicitly disabled. The largest custom system is the mission director: branching encounters, squad assistance, checkpoints and the boss state machine. WebGPU startup and browser validation were the hardest integration points. I would overhaul backend selection to expose a clear public WebGPU-only option.

Prompt feedback

Version: 404299fcd4. The controller-first vertical slice and required attract mode give a clear product target. “Engine-native art” is ambiguous for Three.js, which has no default art assets. Building and validating a complete mission is the hardest part. The most valuable additional test would be a full playthrough with a physical PS5 controller.
