OPERATION AFTERTASTE

You are ROOK. Palisade Logistics has blocked food shipments at Frostline Station. Its most guarded cargo is MSG: monosodium glutamate. Find the keycard. Copy the food ledger and upload a release command. Then reach the boat.

GET THE KEYCARDThe west guard office holds a level-one card.
COPY THE LEDGER / RELEASE THE FOODUse the terminal in the second room. Hold E until both transfers finish.
SURVIVE THE TRANSMISSIONControl will call after the transfer. Then return to the southeast boat.

The long call has 20 chapters and about 8,200 spoken words. It stops the mission clock. You can close it and resume from the archive. Real history is sourced. The military plot is fiction.

All models, textures, dialogue, and sound are generated for MSG. This is an independent fan-style tribute. It is not an official Metal Gear game.

Approach a guard from behind. Press E at close range for a silent takedown. A tranquilizer shot puts a guard to sleep. Sleeping guards can wake up or be found. You can complete the mission without firing.

Movement is screen-aligned. North is up. Hold E to transfer data. Cover blocks sight and shots. Running makes sound. Guards search the last place they saw you. A box does not hide you after a guard sees you enter it.
