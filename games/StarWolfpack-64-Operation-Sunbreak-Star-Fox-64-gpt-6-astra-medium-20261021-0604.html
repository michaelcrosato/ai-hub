About the flight.

STARWOLFPACK 64 / OPERATION SUNBREAK

An original arcade rail-flight sortie inspired by StarFox 64. Built for a standard-mapping controller; keyboard supported. All ships, worlds, sound effects, and music are original procedural work. Not affiliated with Nintendo.
