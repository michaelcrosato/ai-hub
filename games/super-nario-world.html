The feather: Run until the meter is full. Jump to take off. Hold Jump to glide. Tap Run to swing the cape.

Your green friend: Jump onto Pip to ride. Press Run to eat enemies. Press Spin to jump off. To carry a shell or key on foot, keep Run held. Release Run to throw it.

The game fills the browser window. It resizes when the window changes. The art stays in proportion. Wider windows show more of the course.

Gamepad: D-pad or left stick to move, A to jump, X or B to run, Y to spin, LB to drop the reserve item, and Start to pause. Touch controls appear on touch devices.
