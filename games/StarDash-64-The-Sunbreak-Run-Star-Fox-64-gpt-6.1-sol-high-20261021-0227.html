MISSION 01 / ORIS COAST

The Sunbreak Run

A stolen solar carrier is draining Oris’s ocean shield. Fly the coast, find a way through the blockade, and silence the Heliophage before sunrise.

Your wings. Your way.

Move with the left stick or WASD. Hold × / Space to fire and charge a target lock; release for a homing burst. Roll through enemy fire with L1/R1 or Q/E.

Collect gold rings to restore shields. Cyan cores upgrade your weapons. In free flight, steer to turn and use △ / R for a quick U-turn.

Flight tips. Hold the reticle over a target to charge a lock. Rolling grants a short shield and reflects nearby shots. Boost, brake and rolls share energy. Wing support recharges in 24 seconds and repairs your shield. Green rings heal; three rings grant a bomb. Stay close to the beacon during free flight.

Left route: dodge canyon pylons for an upgrade. Right route: destroy the courier’s pursuers. All-range flight uses the left stick to turn your ship; the radar points forward. The carrier’s shield nodes must fall before its core takes damage.
