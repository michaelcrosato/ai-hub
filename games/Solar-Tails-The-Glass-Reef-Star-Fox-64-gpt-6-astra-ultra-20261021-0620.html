AN ORIGINAL LOVE LETTER TO RAIL SHOOTERS

Behind the wings.

SOLAR TAILS is an original game inspired by the on-rails flight, squad camaraderie, branching routes, and dramatic bosses of StarFox 64. All models, characters, scenery, effects, music, and code are original to this demo. No source-game assets are included.

Source information

Date: 2026-10-21 13:44 UTC

Source: StarFox 64

Tech: Three.js 0.180.0 (r180), WebGPU only with fallback disabled; @dimforge/rapier3d-compat 0.19.3; Web Audio API; Gamepad API; JavaScript ES modules; HTML/CSS.

Art Direction: Original low-poly retro-futurist coastal aviation: turquoise seas, terraced rust mesas, ivory and coral spacecraft, geometric clouds, and orbital architecture.

Animation: Procedural transforms and sinusoidal flight paths, rotating boss machinery, camera banking and barrel rolls, Rapier rigid-body debris, mesh particle bursts, animated ocean vertices, and storm rain.

Testing: Two comprehensive browser suites and one targeted regression: 173 assertion executions; all 90 main checks verified, targeted regression 12/12 passed. Engine feedback

Liked most: Three.js makes procedural geometry, materials, cameras, and scene composition concise; Rapier supplies dependable collision queries and rigid-body motion.

Got stuck or guessed wrong: Three.js normally supplies a WebGL fallback, which required explicit removal. Headless WebGPU needed SwiftShader Vulkan flags. Reusing a Rapier world over repeated mission resets exposed a crash; recreating the world and event queue per sortie passed the reset stress test.

Most important custom system: The mission director: timeline encounters, route choice, checkpoint state, boss phases, and a complete controller-driven UI.

Hardest part: Coordinating fixed-step collision events, body lifetimes across retries, controller polling, and rendering while preserving responsive arcade combat.

Remove or overhaul: Make renderer fallback policy explicit instead of requiring an internal callback override for strict WebGPU-only delivery.

One engine change: Add a documented strict-WebGPU initialization mode with clear device-loss diagnostics.
