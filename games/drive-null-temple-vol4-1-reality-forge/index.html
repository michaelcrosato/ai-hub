CONTROLS & HOW TO PLAY

Move with WASD. Aim with the mouse. Hold the left mouse button to fire. You can also aim and fire with the arrow keys.

Space or Shift: dash. E or right click: rupture. G: Last Light limit break. 1–3: weapons. Q: next weapon. V: effects. M: mute. F: full screen.

Gamepad: sticks move and aim. A/LB: dash. B/RB: rupture. X: weapon. Y/LT: skills. D-pad up: repair. D-pad down: interact. View/Select: Last Light. Menu: pause.

Touch: drag anywhere on the left half to move. Drag on the right half to aim and fire. The controls are invisible. Tap the ability buttons to dash or rupture.

Clear each wave. Choose a live upgrade before the timer ends. Pillars stop movement, shots, and boss beams. Press T to change dimensions. Collect green health pickups. Chain kills to trigger overdrive. A boss appears every fourth wave.

The soundtrack is synthesized in your browser. It uses no recordings.
