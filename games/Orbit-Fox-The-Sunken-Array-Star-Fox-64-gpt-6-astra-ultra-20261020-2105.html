Liked most: TSL’s composable materials made the cel-shaded palette straightforward, and the scene graph suited original procedural models.
Stuck or guessed wrong: Three installs an automatic WebGL fallback; this build explicitly disables it and verifies the backend. Headless Linux WebGPU screenshots needed headed Xvfb capture.
Most important custom system: The mission director, encounter data, flight controls, aim assistance, checkpoint, scoring and controller navigation.
Hardest part: Coordinating collision sensors, fast projectiles, camera and actor lifetime across corridor and all-range modes.
