FIELD MANUAL

One base. Four fronts.

Keep the command core alive through 12 waves. Enemies cross the full map. They attack troops, break walls, and reach the core through gaps.

BUILD & DEFEND

Use the bottom bar or keys 1–8. Place extractors on amber deposits. Generators supply power. Field stations extend your build area and heal nearby troops.

Select a building. Use U to upgrade or H to repair. Select a barracks and use T to train four troops for 180 metal.

A power shortage slows weapons, training, and extractors. Each player has a free support strike every 55 seconds.

CONTROL YOUR TROOPS

Click or drag to select. Hold Shift to add troops. Right click to move or attack an enemy. Hold Shift and right click to attack while you move.

Tab selects your troops. Ctrl + 1–4 saves a control group. Alt + 1–4 selects that group. F aims a support strike.

WASD moves the camera. Q / E rotates. Scroll to zoom. Middle-drag to pan. M shows the map. Space returns to the core. P or Esc pauses.

CONTROLLER

Left stick moves the cursor, right stick pans (click it to return to the core). A selects, builds, or orders the selected troops; hold A on open ground to drag a selection box. X attacks while moving. Y selects your troops (rotates a structure while placing). B cancels or clears the selection.

LB / RB rotate, LT / RT zoom. D-pad ◀ ▶ picks a structure, ▲ aims the support strike, ▼ changes speed. View shows the map, Start pauses. Point at any button and press A to use it.

TOUCH

Drag to pan and pinch to zoom. Tap a soldier or structure to select it. With troops selected, tap the ground to move or an enemy to attack. Tap a selected soldier again to select the squad around it.

To build, pick a structure, tap to move the preview, then tap the same spot again or press BUILD HERE. The support strike works the same way.

ENEMIES. Raiders and runners rush the gaps. Brutes break walls. Siege walkers shell structures from 32 m. From wave 4, sappers ignore your troops and blow up the first structure they reach. Every sixth wave a juggernaut assault walker leads the column.
ALLIES. Metal, power, buildings, and the core are shared. Three allied guard squads hold the other fronts. They fight, but do not build or spend metal. Solo games save every 20 seconds and at the start of each build phase.
