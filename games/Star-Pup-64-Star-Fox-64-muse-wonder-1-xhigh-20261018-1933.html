HOW TO PLAY

Fly the Pup-Wing on rails through Corneria. Shoot fighters, turrets & asteroids. Fly through gold rings to repair.
• Laser: tap/hold Cross or Space. Charged shot: hold 0.4s, release — homing blast (locks on).
• Barrel roll: Circle / Q / E or double-tap stick left/right — deflects enemy fire, brief invulnerability.
• Boost / Brake: R2/Shift (fast, drains boost) • L2/Ctrl (slow, recharges faster).
• Smart bomb: Triangle / B — huge AoE, limited stock, pick up more from supply boxes.
• Boss: At the canyon end the rail opens to ALL-RANGE MODE. Orbit the Granga Pup-Tank, destroy its 4 leg cores, then its head core.
• Wingmates Falco, Slippy & Peppy call out tips — keep Slippy alive for bonus hits. Gamepad rumble on damage (if supported).
