THE FINE PRINT

About & credits

Saturn Boom Circuit is an original fan-made spiritual successor to Saturn Bomberman. No original game assets are used.

Source information
Date: 2026-10-29 14:01
Source: Saturn Bomberman
Tech: Three.js r180 WebGPU; Rapier 0.19.3; Gamepad API; AI Hub parallel mode.
Art: procedural cel-shaded 3D. Animation: procedural transforms. Audio: synthesized music and sound effects; no voice.
Online: AI Hub parallel score session when launched in a compatible hub; no direct multiplayer outside the hub.
Testing: lightweight browser and viewport checks. Environment: Codex CLI (headless), Linux; browser unknown. Model: OpenAI gpt-6.1-terra, reasoning effort high. Usage: unknown.
