HOW TO PLAY

W A S D / ARROW KEYSMove. Build speed to run.
SPACEJump. Jump again as you land
for a double or triple jump.
SHIFT / ZCrouch. Press in the air
to ground-pound.
SHIFT + SPACELong jump while running.
Backflip while stopped.
X / JPunch. Pick up or throw.
Press while running to dive.
ETalk. Read signs. Enter cannon.
Use W / S to climb trees.
DRAG / Q / RTurn the camera.
Press C to center it.
ESC / MPause / mute sound.
Jump against walls to kick off.

Gamepad: left stick to move, A to jump, B to attack, left trigger to crouch, right stick for the camera.
