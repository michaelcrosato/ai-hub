GAMEPAD: stick / D-pad to move · X / Y: punches · A / B: kicks · LB / RB: sidestep · LT: throw · RT: launcher · START: pause · menus: A confirms, B goes back.
PS PAD: □ / △ punches · × / ○ kicks.   LOCAL 2P: arrow keys · 7 / 8: punches · 9 / 0: kicks · Right Ctrl: throw · , / .: sidestep. Numpad 4 / 5 / 1 / 2 / 0 also work. A gamepad plays player two in local versus.
TOUCH: drag anywhere on the left half to move (push up to hop, down to crouch) · LP / RP / LK / RK and THROW on the right · STEP ↑ / STEP ↓, LIFT and Ⅱ along the top.
PRACTICE: T (gamepad R3) changes the dummy between idle, guard, and fight. R (gamepad BACK) resets health and position. Neutral guard blocks high and mid attacks.
THE TOURNAMENT: three stages. Clear the Ember Temple at dusk and at night, then face SHO, the Iron Leaf Master, on the Jade Terrace. A cleared stage is saved: CONTINUE ARCADE picks the run back up.
