Finish three laps. Pass 39 cars. Each named rival has a separate battle. Reach each checkpoint before time runs out.
CONTROLS

RIVALS: Once you reach roughly half a car length from a named rival, he locks to about 10 km/h above your normal top speed and keeps blocking. Use a passing move to change the speed balance until you clear him: draft, slingshot, deke high/low, bump, rear-corner clip, side ram, wall ram, or a traffic chain hit. Get your whole car clear to win.
CAMERAS: cycle Close Chase, Bumper, and Far Chase.

Brake before a tight turn. Steer into the bend. Tap the brake to start a slide, then release it and apply power. Steer the other way to end the slide.

Start in 40th. The camera moves from the front of your car to your chosen view. You get control at green. All 39 rivals stay in the race.

Beat the front five. At half a car length, the rival runs about 10 km/h above your normal top speed. A valid passing technique creates a battle-only speed edge until you clear him. Follow close to charge the draft, deke one lane then switch, bump, clip the rear corner, side-ram, or force him into the wall.

Contact moves cars. A rear hit transfers speed. A hit on a rear corner can turn a car. Side contact can push a rival into a wall. A rear hit can also push a car into traffic. Hard contact can slow you too.

Time trial and reverse. TIME TRIAL clears the circuit: no traffic, no clock, three laps against the ghost of your best lap. The centre readout shows how far ahead or behind the ghost you are. REVERSE runs Sunshore Park the other way round, with its own brake boards and records.
