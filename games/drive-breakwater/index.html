01 / THE JOB

Clear the ten marked structures and lower the raised bridge before SUNSPAR arrives. Its integrity drains if it hits a blockage. At zero, the operation fails.

The route plan pauses the action. Once the entire corridor is clear, SUNSPAR accelerates to the dock. Rescue crews and recovery cells are optional during the escort.

02 / THE TOOLS

MULE: ram masonry; hold Space for repeat powered rushes. It cannot break steel or reinforced concrete.

WHIPLASH: drive while holding Space to charge the swinging weight. Release within six metres of steel. A full charge swings automatically. It also breaks masonry.

PILEDRIVER: Space leaps, then slams a wide area. Steer in the air. It breaks reinforced concrete and masonry, but not steel.

04 / SECOND SHIFT

After delivery, choose reclamation to explore without a timer. Clear all 20 structures, rescue all 18 crew and find all 14 recovery cells for the district-completion badge.

Gold service medal: open the route within 2:00, deliver ≥80% hull, rescue all 3 crews and collect ≥8 cells before delivery, on Standard. A checkpoint retry caps the medal at Bronze. Reclamation cannot change the escort medal.

BREAKWATER — Foundry Reach
An original spiritual successor to the demolition-and-escort idea of Blast Corps. Not affiliated with Nintendo, Rare or Microsoft.

Original level, machines, models, interface, music and sound definitions. No source-game art, names, levels or audio are used.

Engine foundation
Riftlight / Pixel Engine by Michael Crosato’s repository. Input, framing, frame scheduling and synthesis adapted from commit eb152243. This build uses a new dependency-free WebGL backend, not the upstream Three.js/Rapier renderer.
