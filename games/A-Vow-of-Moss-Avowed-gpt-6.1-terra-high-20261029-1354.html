Testing feedback
23 viewport checks found no horizontal overflow. One critical test-environment blocker: headless browsers could not obtain WebGPU, so gameplay remains unverified. Most helpful: viewport matrix. Least helpful: an initial invalid device selector.

Engine feedback
Three.js makes procedural world building approachable. WebGPU availability blocked testing. Quest, combat and controller systems needed custom implementation. Browser GPU support was hardest; overhaul compatibility diagnostics and improve consistent WebGPU support.

Prompt feedback
The controller-first direction is useful. The meaning of a complete successor in a single-file demo is ambiguous. The hardest part is the scope. The most valuable further test would be a real PS5 controller multiplayer playthrough.
