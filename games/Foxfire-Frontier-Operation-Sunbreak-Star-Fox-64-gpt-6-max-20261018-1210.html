About & credits

For the joy of the barrel roll.

Foxfire Frontier is an original spiritual successor to Starfox 64 for N64. Fly as Rook of Sunbreak Squadron and stop the imperial Weathermaker from draining the archipelago’s sunlight.

Rook · pilot. Unreasonably optimistic.

Pip · scout. The sensible one, allegedly.

Mica · engineer. Keeps everyone flying.

All aircraft, islands, characters, effects, music and sounds are original and generated in code. No assets from the source game are used. Unofficial; not affiliated with Nintendo.
