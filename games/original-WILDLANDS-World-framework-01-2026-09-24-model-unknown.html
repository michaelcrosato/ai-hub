Framework guide

Source modules: Config, Noise, Terrain, Chunk, World, Lighting, Player, Sites, Wildlife, NightSky, Soundscape, UI, and Game. Use World.addSystem(system) to add an update(dt, game) hook. Listen for biomechange, chunkload, modechange, and discover on game.events. Access the running app through window.game; window.Wildlands holds the modules and THREE. Terrain.sample(x,z) returns height, region, and color. Change Config.seed for a different world. Version 1 includes terrain collision and a survey of twenty-four sites. Prop collision, combat, inventory, and multiplayer are not included. It renders with WebGPU and falls back to WebGL2.
