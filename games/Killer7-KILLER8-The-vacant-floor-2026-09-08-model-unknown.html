COMBAT: You cannot move while you aim. Laughter warns you of a hidden enemy. Scan to reveal it.
Shoot the bright red point for a one-shot kill and more blood. Shoot legs to slow an enemy.
A twin splits in two unless its red point takes it down. Thin blood restores health or powers skills. Thick blood buys upgrades in Room 08.

MENUS: arrows, W / S or the D-pad move. ENTER, E or A confirms. ESC or B goes back.
Graphics quality, touch layout and vibration: SETTINGS → GRAPHICS & CONTROLS, or F9.

KILLER8 / THE VACANT FLOOR

A FAMILIAR BAD DREAM.

This is an unofficial, original browser-game homage to the art and play of Killer7. The tower mission takes its main cues from the Celtic Building opening. It does not use the original game's art, music, code, characters, or dialogue.

Built as one HTML file on three.js: WebGPU where the browser has it, WebGL2 where it does not. Procedural 3D art.
Original sound made in Web Audio. No network connection or install is needed.

Killer7 belongs to its respective owners. This project is not linked to or approved by them.
Contains stylized violence, sudden sounds, and flashing effects. Reduced flashes are available in Settings.
