BUILD THE KICK

Bring all four sleepers into the lift. Secure them. Fit the impulse unit. Release the brake. Match the signal, then trigger the kick. Their security will not make it easy.

The small van display is not decoration. It shows why gravity changes. Hold a brace when a turn warning appears. Five memory fragments are hidden on this level. Endurance, on the title screen, keeps the hall fight going for as long as you last.
