01 / FOLLOW THE RETICLE

Steering aims your guns. Targets near the sight receive gentle aim assistance. Hold Square / X until the target reads LOCKED, then release a seeking blast.

02 / ROLL THROUGH TROUBLE

Barrel rolls briefly protect your hull and reflect hostile fire. Rolling, boosting, and braking share a recharging thrust reserve.

03 / FLY THE GOLD LINE

Gold rings restore shields. Three rings upgrade your twin lasers. Cyan supplies restore shields and bombs. A full shield supply becomes bonus score.

04 / LOOK AFTER YOUR PEOPLE

Destroy the ace chasing Pip. Cover orders focus wingmen on enemies. Rally orders bring them close and slowly repair their ships. Both pilots return at sector checkpoints.

THE PEOPLE, PIXELS & PARTICULARS

Behind the flight.

FOXTROT 64 / THE SUNBREAK RUN
An original spiritual successor inspired by StarFox 64. No Nintendo characters, music, models, or extracted game assets are used. All ships, scenery, portraits, effects, and music are created procedurally for this demo.
