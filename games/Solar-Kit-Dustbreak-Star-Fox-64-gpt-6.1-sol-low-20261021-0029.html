VANGUARD FIELD GUIDE

Own the sky.

Steer within the flight corridor. Tap or hold R2 / Space for twin lasers. Keep a target inside the sight for a charged homing shot. Barrel rolls reflect incoming fire; boost and brake change your pace. Nova bombs clear nearby threats. Fly through gold rings to restore shields and earn points.

At the fork, fly left for the perilous refinery route or right for the supply route. Your squad marks the route you chose. Break the carrier's two side engines, then destroy its exposed core. A three-minute sortie ends with a scored debrief. A destroyed fighter can retry immediately.
