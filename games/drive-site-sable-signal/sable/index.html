EVERY TOOL COUNTS. Guns have fixed ammunition. An empty gun is still a useful throw. A bare-hand strike knocks an enemy down.

READ THE ROOM. Closed doors block shots and sight. Gunfire draws nearby guards. A red aim line warns of a shot.

CONTROL THE PACE. Signal brake slows guards and their shots for 2.4 seconds. Three kills restore one charge. It does not make you invincible.

LINK YOUR ATTACKS. Each kill within 4 seconds grows the chain. Mix melee, guns, throws, doors, and finishes for a style bonus.
