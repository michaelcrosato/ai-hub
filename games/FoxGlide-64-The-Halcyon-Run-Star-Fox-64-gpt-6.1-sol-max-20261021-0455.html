Know your interceptor

Flight manual

Controller first. Keyboard always welcome.
Forward flight is automatic.

L / WASD or arrows

Find your line

Steer across the flight corridor. Your guns follow your aim reticle.

✕ / Shift

Boost

Hold to surge ahead. It uses flight energy, which recharges automatically.

L2 / Ctrl

Airbrake

Hold to slow your approach, line up a ring, or buy a little breathing room.

L1R1 / Q or E

Barrel roll

Tap either shoulder to roll. You are protected and reflect nearby enemy bolts.

OPTIONS / Esc or P

Take a breath

Pause at any moment. Disconnected controllers pause the sortie, too.

GOLD RINGS / REPAIR CACHES

Stay in the sky

Rings restore shield and energy. Every third ring increases shield capacity.

Move the stick up to climb. If either axis feels reversed, Settings has separate horizontal and vertical inversion toggles.

R2 / Space

Blasters

Hold to fire continuously. A little aim assistance keeps close shots satisfying.

HOLD → RELEASE

Charged seekers

Hold fire until the reticle turns gold. Line up a target, then release for a homing shot.

△ / X

Nova bomb

Three to start. Clears enemy fire and hits the whole formation, including boss pylons.

□ / R

Squadron strike

Call a coordinated attack. It recharges in 26 seconds; saving Rook makes it faster.

5 SECOND CHAIN WINDOW

Build a streak

Rapid eliminations multiply points. Reach 3, 8, and 15 kills for ×2, ×3, and ×4.

BLUE CACHE / TWIN BLASTERS

Upgrade your wings

Blue pickups double your guns. Green crosses fully repair your shields.

Warden protocol: destroy both glowing wing pylons to expose the center core. Red beam telegraphs mark where to move away from before the weapon fires.
