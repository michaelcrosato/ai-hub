Engine Feedback

What worked best? Three.js makes procedural geometry and material composition straightforward. Rapier provides a clear, robust collision and ray-query layer.
What caused friction? r180 automatically installs a WebGL fallback; this demo explicitly disables it before initialization. Code inspection also prompted replacing custom prism faces with ExtrudeGeometry for consistent face winding.
Most important custom system? The mission director: combat, squadmates, branching route, checkpoints, and a reusable attract pilot.
Hardest part? Synchronizing fast projectile queries and fixed-step physics with responsive flight controls.
What would I overhaul? Make backend fallback an explicit application policy.
One engine change? A supported require-WebGPU option, with a clear startup diagnostic.
