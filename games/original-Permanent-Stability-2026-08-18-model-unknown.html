How to Govern Meridia

Each month

Commission programs in the selected district and authorize research, then advance one month. Income, research and every trend on the stats bar apply once per month.

The race

Research climbs to General Intelligence, Synthetic Security and the Permanent Stability Protocol. Its thresholds need high control, autonomy and robot reliability with manageable unrest and a working grid.

Ways to lose

Unrest at 100%, legitimacy gone without enough control, a treasury deep in debt, or an intelligence that reaches full autonomy before the protocol is secured.
