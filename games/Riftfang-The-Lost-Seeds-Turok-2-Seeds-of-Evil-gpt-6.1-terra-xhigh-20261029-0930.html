The story behind the hunt

About / credits

Riftfang: The Lost Seeds is an original game inspired by the atmosphere and exploration of Turok 2: Seeds of Evil.

Source information
Date: 2026-10-29 09:30
Tech: Three.js r180 WebGPU, Rapier 0.19.3, Web Audio, Gamepad API, AI Hub Session Protocol 1.3.0, BroadcastChannel.
Art: procedural cel-shaded lost world. Animation: procedural transforms. Audio: synthesized music, effects and gibberish voice.
Online: AI Hub side-by-side sessions and same-origin tab score challenge.
Testing: headless Chrome 154 with Vulkan/SwiftShader, keyboard/menu and gameplay checks; no FPS target. Five issues caught, one critical. Most helpful: rendered browser interaction. Least helpful: an incompatible headless ANGLE setting.
Environment: Codex CLI (headless); Linux; browser unknown at authoring.
Model: OpenAI gpt-6.1-terra, reasoning effort xhigh. Usage: unknown.

Engine feedback
Three.js makes procedural worlds flexible. The difficult work was integrating rendering, physics and controller UX; the game loop, enemy AI and audio were built for this slice. I initially chose a headless graphics setting that produced blank WebGPU captures; a different Vulkan setting resolved it. WebGPU availability makes testing harder. I would overhaul implicit renderer fallback into an explicit capability decision, with a clearer error API.

Prompt feedback
The creative constraints and controller-first direction were useful. The boundary between a complete engine and a vertical slice was ambiguous. Integrating the whole experience was the hardest part. A long real-device DualSense and multiplayer playthrough would be the most useful additional test.
