About & Credits

StarFoxels is an original, playful riff on StarFox 64: free flight with on-rails pacing, wingmen, radio chatter, a branching route, and a three-phase boss. Everything is drawn and synthesized in the browser.

Controls: left stick or WASD to fly. Hold cross or R2 to fire; a full charge launches homing shots. Circle or Q/E to barrel roll. Triangle or X for a smart bomb. R1 boosts, L1 brakes.
