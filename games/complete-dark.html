Briefing / clearance 07

A quiet way in.

Asterion has built a weapon that listens. Tonight, its research archive goes to a private buyer. You will reach the tower by air and enter through the roof.

Disable the rooftop relay to open the archive locks. Enter the tower, copy the NIGHTFALL files, then return to the landing pad. The security room has spare equipment. Service passages give you another route.

This is an original, unofficial homage to console spy shooters of the late 1990s. The setting, geometry, textures, music, and sound effects are original. No game assets or external services are used.
