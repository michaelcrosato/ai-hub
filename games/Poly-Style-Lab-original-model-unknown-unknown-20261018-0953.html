Find a look your assets can support.

This lab keeps the same simple geometry in every preset. There are no imported models, image textures, or extra detail added by a filter.

Start with Storybook

Try the split view. Turn ink, shadows, and toon light off one at a time. Keep the effects that improve the scene.

Test both scenes

A look must work on trees and terrain, but also on boxes, thin beams, and overlapping parts.

Check the moving view

Use Orbit to test lines and print patterns in motion. Screen-space ink can break on thin or distant shapes.

Save the result

Export a PNG or a JSON recipe. The recipe includes the settings, camera, palette, and a brief for an asset-building agent.

What a filter cannot fix

Bad proportions, unclear shapes, gaps, poor scene layout, and broken animation still need asset or game changes. Contact shade and glow here are simple screen-space effects, not full production AO or HDR bloom. Print and grain are fixed to the screen.
