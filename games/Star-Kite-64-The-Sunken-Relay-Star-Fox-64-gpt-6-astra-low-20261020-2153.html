Flight manual / infrastructure

The mission director runs timed encounter data, two route variants, radio cues and checkpoint snapshots. Combat uses a 60 Hz Rapier collision world. Pause freezes the simulation; losing focus pauses live flight. Controller disconnection pauses for keyboard recovery. Local storage keeps preferences and the best score. The title launches an autonomous attract flight after 10 seconds, with infinite resources and accelerated combat; any input returns to the hangar.

WebGPU and a secure browser context are required. CDN connectivity is required for the pinned libraries; no WebGL fallback is provided. Serve this file over HTTPS or localhost. Documentation: Three.js WebGPU · Rapier collision events.
