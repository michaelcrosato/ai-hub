W A S DSteer. W adds speed. S brakes.
SPACEHold to crouch. Release to ollie.
JFlip. Add a direction for more tricks.
KHold a grab while in the air.
LHold near a rail to grind.
IHold on landing to start a manual.
Q / ESpin left / right in the air.
SHIFTAir special. Fill the special meter.
RReturn to the start. Lose the combo.
ESC / MPause / sound. F for fullscreen.

Link tricks to build a multiplier. Jump from a rail. Hold I on landing. Then jump into the next trick. Balance with A / D on rails and W / S in manuals. Release a grab before landing. A late flip or lost balance causes a bail.

Gamepad: Left stick to steer · A to ollie · X to flip · B to grab · Y to grind · LB / RB to spin · LT for manual · RT for special · Start to pause.
