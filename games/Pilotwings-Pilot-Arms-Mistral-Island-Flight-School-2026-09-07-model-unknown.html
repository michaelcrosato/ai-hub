    MISTRAL ISLAND FLIGHT CLUB

    Your flight manual.

    Keep it steady. Bring it home.

    Fly through five gold rings. Land on the long runway and stop. The next ring is bright gold. A green ring means a hit; a red ring means a miss.

    ← → or A DBank and turn.
    ↑ ↓ or W SUp lowers the nose. Down raises it.
    Q E or Z XReduce / increase engine power.
    SPACEHold to slow down. Hold after landing to stop.
    On final approach, hold Space. Stay above the runway center line. Keep your wings level. With flight aid on, the air brake also gives a gentle descent and a soft flare near the ground. In free flight, add full power and raise the nose to take off.

    The ground is your target.

    Fall through three flat rings. Open your parachute after the last ring. Steer to the red-and-white target west of the runway.

    ← → or A DMove left / right.
    ↑ ↓ or W SMove forward / back across the island.
    SPACEOpen your parachute. It cannot close again.
    Open your chute while the height is above 100 ft. The camera always faces north. After the chute opens, use short key presses to aim at the center of the target. Release the keys to slow your sideways motion.

    A little thrust goes a long way.

    Lift off from the small pad. Fly through three gold rings. Land on the large yellow target north of the start pad.

    ← → or A DTurn left / right.
    ↑ ↓ or W SMove forward / back.
    SPACEHold to rise. Release to fall.
    SHIFTHover and slow down. Uses fuel.
    Use short bursts of thrust to set your height. Hold Shift before landing to slow down. Release it to descend. Get close to the center of the yellow pad, then make a soft landing. With flight aid on, hold Shift below 45 ft for a slow final descent.

    Common keys: Esc / P: pause · R: restart · M: sound · F: full screen · H: manual.
    Gamepad: Left stick: steer · A: brake / chute / thrust · B: hover · LB / RB: power · Start: pause. Gamepad support depends on the browser.
    Earn 210 points across all three tests for a Class A license. Best scores and settings save on this browser when storage is available. This is an arcade game, not real flight training. All art and music are original.
