THE LITTLE RULEBOOK

01 / GET TO THE BALL

You are the blue player at the bottom. Move with the arrow keys or W A S D. The small ring shows where the ball will bounce. Stay behind it to meet the ball after the bounce.

02 / TWO BUTTONS. PLENTY OF GAME.

Hold Z or Space to hit when the ball is in reach. Tap just as it arrives for extra power. Hold X to lob over a player at the net. Hold left or right as you hit to aim. Hold up for a deep shot, or down for a short shot.

03 / MAKE YOUR SERVE COUNT

Hold Z for a safe serve. For more control, tap once to toss, then tap again near the top. Land the ball in the marked box. You get two tries. A net touch that lands in the correct box is a let: serve again.

04 / GAME, SET, ONE MORE MATCH

Points go 0, 15, 30, 40, game. At deuce, win two points in a row. Win a quick set with 3 games, or a full set with 6. Lead by two games. At 3–3 or 6–6, play a tie-break: first to 7 points, win by two. Practice has no score.

05 / TAKE A BREAK

P or Esc pauses. M switches sound. F switches full screen. On a touch screen, use the on-screen buttons. A connected gamepad also works: left stick to move, A to hit, B to lob, Start to pause.
