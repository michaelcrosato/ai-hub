GET STARTED

A few simple moves.

There is no goal. Add objects and explore how they move. For a goal, pick a challenge from the scene menu.

Add a shapeChoose a shape, then press Drop. Or press 1, 2, or 3. Enter drops the selected shape.
Move and throwDrag an object. Release it to let go. Scroll while you drag to change its depth.
Change your viewDrag empty space to orbit. Right-drag or Shift-drag to pan. Scroll to zoom. WASD pan, the arrow keys orbit, + and − zoom.
Take your timePress Space to pause. Press . to advance one step. Resume to drag an object.

Aim and carryThe left stick moves the cursor. Hold A on a shape to carry it and let go to throw. A on the floor drops your shape there.
BuildX drops the selected shape. LT switches shape. Y opens the controls panel: D-pad to choose, left and right to change, A to press.
Change your viewThe right stick orbits and the D-pad pans. LB and RB zoom, or push and pull a carried shape. R3 resets the view.
Take your timeStart pauses. RT advances one step while paused. View opens this help and B cancels.
Add a shapeTap Add to open the shape panel, then Drop. Or turn on Click to place and tap the floor.
Move and throwDrag an object with one finger. Lift your finger to let go.
Change your viewDrag empty space to orbit. Pinch to zoom. Drag with two fingers to pan.
Take your timeThe toolbar pauses, steps, changes the speed and resets the scene.

Challenges: Bowling lane asks for all ten pins in as few throws as you can; let go of the ball to bowl, and after each throw the fallen pins are cleared and the ball comes back. Plank tower asks you to pull planks out, or lay them on top, without toppling it. Best results count at Earth gravity and stay in this browser, like your world.
