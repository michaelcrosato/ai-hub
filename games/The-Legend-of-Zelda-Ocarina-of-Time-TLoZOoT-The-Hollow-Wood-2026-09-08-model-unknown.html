A small legend

Find the seed sling in the old wood. Enter the temple and restore its three ward flames.

Watch your foe. Block a strike, then answer with your sword. Roll through a wave of roots.

Gold buds react to seeds. Blue shrines restore hearts and seeds. Cut grass and break pots for supplies.

The western court holds a flute. The eastern tablet holds its song. An arch east of the Old Wood hides a grove of light sleepers.

Gamepad: Left stick moves. Right stick turns the camera. A rolls. X attacks. Y fires. B uses. LT locks on. RT shields. LB opens the flute; the D-pad and A play notes. RB zooms, RS centers the camera. Start pauses. Back mutes.

Touch: The stick appears under your left thumb. Sword, Roll, Shield, Sling and Lock sit on the right, with Use when something is near. Drag the open view to turn; pinch to zoom. Lock with nothing nearby recenters the camera. Flute and II sit at the top right.
