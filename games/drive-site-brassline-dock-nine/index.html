THE CORNER BOOK

Timing beats force.

Watch Rook’s gloves. Make him miss. Strike while his guard is open.

01

Choose your target

J / K punch the body. Hold W to aim high. U / I also punch the head. Change hands for a damage bonus.

02

Move at the right time

Tap A / D to dodge. Tap S for a sweep. Release, then tap again for the next strike. Hold SHIFT to guard against hooks.

03

Take the opening

Wait until the whole attack ends. Clean hits and dodges build power. At full power, press SPACE for a Break punch.

04

Stay in the fight

Rest between attacks to restore energy. If you fall, hold SPACE to rise before ten. Three knockdowns end the bout. Beat the three-minute clock.

THE COUNTER WINDOW

A small teal flash marks a brief chance to interrupt a strike. Hit the body during a hook. Hit the head during a body drive.

TOUCH

Use the left panel to dodge, duck, or hold guard. Use the right panel to choose a hand and a target. Tap BREAK at full power. You can hold guard with one finger and use another control with a second finger.

GAMEPADStick / D-pad: dodge and duck · X / A: body · Y / B: head · LB: guard · RB: Break · Start: pause
