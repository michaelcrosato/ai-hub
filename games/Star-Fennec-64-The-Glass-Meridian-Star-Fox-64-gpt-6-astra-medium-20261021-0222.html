Choose a gate at the canyon fork. Protect your wingmate for a bonus. At the carrier, destroy both engine pods, then the exposed core. A red line means a beam is charging: move before it fires.

Flight is on rails. You can move within the corridor, boost, brake, and choose a route. No free roaming. A sortie lasts roughly four minutes.

About this little universe.

Star Fennec 64 is an original, procedural arcade flight game inspired by StarFox 64. All ships, environments, characters, music and effects are generated in this file. No Nintendo assets or characters are used.

Engine feedback

Canvas offers direct control and zero dependencies. Rendering, combat, controller navigation, mission scripting and audio were built here. Coherent perspective and readable combat were the hardest parts. No runtime observations are available; tuning remains unverified. For a larger game, I would replace painter sorting with depth-buffer rendering. Native depth-aware primitives would simplify the engine.

Prompt feedback · 404299fcd4

The single-file deliverable and controller-first direction were useful constraints. “Unlimited performance” leaves the practical content budget unclear.
