ARCADE BOXING   /  
YOUR CORNER. YOUR RULES.

LEARN THE ROPES.

Knock Buster down three times before the three-minute clock runs out. Watch his wind-up. Avoid his punch. Hit back while his guard is down.

01 / FIND THE OPENING

Press J or K for a body punch. Hold ↑ or W to aim at his head. Hit where his gloves are not. Alternate hands for better combos.

02 / MAKE HIM MISS

Tap ← / → or A / D to dodge. Tap ↓ or S to duck a hook. Your boxer returns to the center. Release the key before you dodge again.

03 / BRING THE RIOT

Hits and dodges fill the Riot Meter. At full power, press SPACE for a heavy punch. A late dodge gives bonus power. A counter punch hits harder.

04 / STAY ON YOUR FEET

Hold SHIFT to block a jab. Heavy punches break a block. Stop punching to restore stamina. If you fall, tap J and K to get up. If he falls, tap to regain health.

TOUCH: Use the buttons below the ring. Hold ↑ with one finger and tap a punch with the other.
MORE KEYS: Z / X = punch · P / Esc = pause · M = sound · F = full screen
HANDMADE FOR THE RING

RING RIOT!

An original arcade boxing game inspired by the timing, large characters, and bright rings of 16-bit boxing games. Buster Brick, the artwork, the music, and the game code were made for this game. No sprites, music, logos, or characters were taken from a Nintendo game.

One complete bout: three enemy phases, two skill levels, head and body attacks, counters, dodges, ducking, blocks, stamina, power punches, knockdowns, a ten-count, saved scores, and rematches.

Graphics: Three.js WebGL, with a Canvas 2D fallback.

Three.js is loaded from a public CDN. The game starts in Canvas mode if the network or WebGL is unavailable. All game art, fonts, and sounds are included in this HTML. No account, tracking, or game server is used. Best scores are stored on this device when local storage is available.
