DRIVER CHECK

Use the GPU driver supplied through Samsung system updates. Update your browser. Do not install a random “gaming driver.” Check chrome://gpu for browser GPU status. WebGPU needs HTTPS or localhost.

Driver versions and GPU temperature are not exposed by this app. This build has not been measured on a physical S26.

The render test uses a fixed camera route. It is not a full gameplay benchmark.

SURVIVE THE VAULT

MOVE. AIM. FIRE.

TOUCH

Drag the left area to move. Drag the right area to aim. Hold the large crosshair button to fire. Use the arrow to jump. Use the double arrow to dash. Tap the swap button to change weapons.

DESKTOP + GAMEPAD

Use WASD to move and the mouse to aim. Click to fire. Space jumps. Shift runs. Q dashes. Keys 1–3 change weapons. Esc pauses. A gamepad uses the left stick to move, the right stick to aim, the right trigger to fire, and A to jump.

THE MISSION

Clear three waves. Stay out of the lava. Find health, armor, and ammo. Then move to the blue gate. There is one secret in the arena.

YOUR QUAKE DATA

Load a local pak0.pak file to explore Quake BSP29 maps with original textures. The map explorer does not run QuakeC. Doors, lifts, original monsters, and scripted level progress are not implemented. Press N or tap the up/down button for free flight. Space or Jump moves up. Shift or Dash moves down.

This is an independent Quake-inspired game, not a complete port. Original game data is not included or uploaded.

BRING YOUR OWN SLIPGATE

LOCAL QUAKE DATA.

Choose a PAK file from your Quake installation. Files stay on this device. They are not sent to a server.

Quake assets have separate license terms. Do not include them in a public site without permission.

Map geometry, palette textures, basic baked lighting, and world collision are supported. Game scripts and animated Quake models are not supported.
