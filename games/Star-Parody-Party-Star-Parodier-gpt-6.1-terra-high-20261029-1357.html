About this party

Star Parody Party is an original affectionate riff on Star Parodier. Dodge, collect treats, upgrade your firepower and defeat the Snack Baron. Three ships, two stages, a boss, bombs and a score attack await!

Controls: Left stick / arrows / WASD move; ✕ / Z / Space fires; ○ / X bombs; Options / Esc pauses. Collect P for power, B for a bomb, and hearts for health. Survive to the end to win.

Source information: 2026-10-29 13:57; Star Parodier. Three.js r180 WebGPU, Rapier 0.19.3, Gamepad API, AI Hub 1.3.0. Cel-shaded toybox art; procedural animation; original synthesized music and effects; no voice. Optional AI Hub parallel score sessions. Testing: lightweight viewport/browser checks; real iOS Safari unavailable. Environment: Codex CLI (headless), Linux, browser unknown. Model: OpenAI gpt-6.1-terra, high. Usage: unknown.

Testing feedback: Issue counts and most/least helpful tests: unknown; see delivery report.

Engine feedback: WebGPU brings expressive 3D; browser support and async setup are the hardest parts. The encounters and controller interface were built here. Better capability diagnostics would help.

Prompt feedback: The creative direction is excellent; multiplayer host availability is unclear. The hardest part is a complete arcade slice. A real PS5 controller and iOS Safari test would be valuable.
