FIELD GUIDE / READ THE CONDITIONS

A LITTLE LESS GUESSWORK.

See the city. Read the scope.

Orange marks guards. Cyan marks hostages. Stop all marked guards. Do not hit a hostage. The orange ring shows when a guard will fire. Use cover while you reload. Stop the helicopter by hitting its two orange engine pods.

Night Ferry: yellow marks spotters. When a spotter's ring fills, its lamp finds you and every guard fires early, unless you are in cover. The courier moves between rooftop cover: lead him before his boat is ready. The Grey Heron stops to fire its deck gun. Take cover, then hit its two stern engine pods before it reaches open water.

In Guided mode, the small mint diamond shows the estimated impact at the measured range. Put that diamond on your target. Moving targets can move before the round arrives. The estimate does not include random spread or a later change in wind. In Unassisted mode, the diamond is off.

What the game simulates

Each round moves through the 3D scene. Gravity, changing wind, air drag and flight time affect its path. Temperature, pressure and humidity change air drag. Lower speed reduces impact energy. Buildings, roof equipment and the harbor water stop rounds. Light and haze change what you can see. The low-light optic gives a brighter but noisier image.

Rifle mass, support, breath, fatigue, recoil, heat and tracking speed affect the sight. Ammunition changes speed, wind response, spread and impact. A damped report lowers recoil and guard alert, but also lowers round speed. Scope power changes the view, not the round.

This is a fictional game model. It does not use real weapon or ammunition data. It is not a real-world training or firing tool. Equipment and distances use game units (u).
