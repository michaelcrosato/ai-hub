Release the flight keys to hover. Use rockets against groups. Use guided missiles against heavy armor. Your base has unlimited supplies. All three engineers must get home.

On a touch screen, use the flight stick and the FIRE, WINCH, and FLARE buttons. Weapons aim at the nearest target.

This original game is an unofficial tribute to Nuclear Strike for PlayStation. All graphics and sounds are generated in this file. No network connection is required.
