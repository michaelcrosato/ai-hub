Into the hollow

Explore each floor and defeat its guardian. Use the open gate to descend.

  • Hold click or J to attack. Aim with the pointer. With J, your blade follows your movement.
  • Dodge through attacks with Space. A short gold flash means you are safe from damage.
  • Press Q for a special attack. Each weapon has its own special attack.
  • Press E near a chest, weapon, shrine, or gate. Compare a weapon before you equip it.
  • Spend gold at a forge to improve your weapon.
  • Press H to use a healing flask. Chests can give more flasks.
  • Defeat enemies to gain experience. Select one upgrade at each new level.
  • Use the same seed to make the same floors and starting encounters. Combat and your choices can change the loot.
