Stand close to an object to inspect it. Hold aim to face a nearby enemy. You cannot move while aiming. Combine herbs in the inventory. Use the item box to free space. Read files for puzzle clues.

Gamepad: left stick / D-pad to move; hold LT to aim; RT or A to fire; A to act; LB to run; X to reload; RB to quick turn; Y for inventory; Back for map; Start to pause; B to go back. Touch: the stick moves Mara; hold AIM, then tap ACT to fire.
