01 / Flight

WASD or arrow keys. On a phone, use the left stick. Release the controls to hover. Hold SHIFT or BOOST for speed.

02 / Weapons

Hold SPACE, left mouse, or FIRE. The cannon tracks a nearby target. Point at a target with the mouse to select it. Let the gun cool if it overheats.

03 / Missiles & EMP

Press R or MISSILE for a guided strike. Press Q or EMP to stop nearby enemies and incoming fire. EMP needs 18 seconds to recharge.
