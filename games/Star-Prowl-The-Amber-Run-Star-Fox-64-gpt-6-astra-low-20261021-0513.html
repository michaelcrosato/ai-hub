Flight manual

Blue rings restore 12 shield; gold rings add a nova bomb. Boost breaks pursuit and advances enemies faster. Brake slows the approach and gives aiming time. Barrel rolls deflect bullets. Wing support repairs 22 shield and clears nearby threats, then recharges for 35 seconds. Nova bombs clear bullets and damage every visible enemy. Chain kills within four seconds to increase your score multiplier. The route fork responds to your aircraft position. The high route has extra fighters and rings; the low route has more armor. The carrier opens its reactor after both side cores are destroyed. You can retry the carrier checkpoint after a defeat.

Standard gamepad mapping: × confirm, ○ back, Options pause. Keyboard: arrows / WASD steer, Space fire, X bomb, Q/E roll, Shift boost, Ctrl brake, R support, Esc pause. Aim assist and dead zones are built in. Pointer buttons navigate menus; flight requires keyboard or controller.
