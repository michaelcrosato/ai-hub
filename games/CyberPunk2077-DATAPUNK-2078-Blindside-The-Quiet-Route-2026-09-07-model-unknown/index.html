Hold Tab. Aim at a target. Use the wheel to select a quickhack. Press F to upload it. Press the middle mouse button to tag the target. Tagged targets stay visible behind cover.

Health items and EMP grenades refill over time. Sprinting does not use stamina outside combat. Shots and melee attacks use stamina. Hold a melee attack for a strong strike. Use F behind a guard to grab them, then F for a lethal strike or R for a nonlethal strike.

MERCY STREET: Choose a background and spend two focus points. Start with the weapon holstered. Press F near a contact. During a talk, use the mouse or 1–9 to choose. Use Up/Down and F to confirm. L opens the transcript. Esc leaves. There is no time limit.

Ask Iona and Jax for proof. Vale, Rhee, and Sable grant different access rights. Check I → Character and J → Journal. A signed release can prevent every fight. Harmful actions cancel passes. Taking the core without a signed release or a sensor bridge calls reinforcements.

For the quiet route, use the green Service C7 door on the west side of Mercy Street. Crouch through the two drains. Open the pump handwheel. Bridge the core sensor before taking the core. Return through the low rear hatch. Use Z to lure a guard with a noise bolt. Use H to carry or hide a body. No skill is required.

Keyboard and mouse, a controller or a touch screen all work. The field guide follows the device you used last. G is an extra EMP key for mice without a middle button.
