DOCKING

Approach the station aperture below 90 m/s with the station near the centre of the sight, then press C. A docking computer can take over from 3 km.

OBJECTIVE

Build capital through trade and contracts, upgrade your ship, collect bounties, and explore the 128-system chart. There is no scripted end state.

TOUCH

Hold anywhere on the stick side of the screen (the half without buttons: the left half, or the right half with the mirrored layout in Settings) for a flight stick: up and down pitch, left and right roll. The buttons on the other side fire, launch missiles, boost and set the throttle. The row along the top pauses, opens the chart, jumps, docks, cycles targets and fires the E.C.M.

THREATS

Pirate gunships and warrant targets carry homing missiles. Shoot an incoming missile down, outrun it on boost, or fit an E.C.M. system to destroy every missile nearby. Customs patrols in secure systems scan for restricted cargo. An escape capsule saves your life and your ship's fit, but not your cargo, if the hull fails.

TRADE

Green prices are below the galactic average, amber ones above it. The galaxy chart lists what each system sells cheaply and pays well for. Supply contracts ask you to source goods yourself and deliver them before the deadline.
