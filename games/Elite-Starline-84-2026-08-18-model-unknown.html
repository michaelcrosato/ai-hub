DOCKING

Approach the station aperture below 90 m/s with the station near the centre of the sight, then press C. A docking computer can take over from 3 km.

OBJECTIVE

Build capital through trade and contracts, upgrade your ship, collect bounties, and explore the 128-system chart. There is no scripted end state.
