Energy Is Everything

Your energy bar is both machine integrity and boost fuel. Recharge on green lanes. Boost unlocks after lap one. Yellow-magenta pads add speed without energy cost. Collect green ion rings to repair damage.

Keyboard Mapping

WASD or arrow keys steer and drive. Space boosts. Shift attacks. Z and X side-dash. Q looks back. Escape pauses. R restarts. F1 opens diagnostics. F2 toggles the developer autopilot.

Circuit Systems

The level includes a vertical inversion, magnetic jump, energy lanes, dual-line boost pads, plasma grit, a moving ion gate, tunnel arches, traffic AI, drafting, collisions, lap timing, records, and a persistent ghost.
