MISSION 03 · CARRIER ROUTE

ARGENT TOWERS

A damaged nuclear missile carrier is locked on autopilot and rolling straight through the silver heart of Argent Towers. One bump and the whole district glows. The Blast Corps has minutes to clear a path.

Primary

  • Demolish every building in the carrier's route (7)
  • Don't let the carrier touch anything — or hit it yourself

For 100%

  • Light all 100 RDUs
  • Flatten all 14 buildings & rescue 8 survivors
  • Find 2 comm points, the scientist and the secret

Fleet on site

  • Backlash — power-slide the armoured rear
  • Ramdozer — rams & pushes TNT
  • Subway train, Police car
  • Ballista — missiles & turbo
  • J-Bomb — flight & stomp

Vehicles

BacklashHold Action (or brake) while steering at speed to power-slide. Only the armoured rear smashes buildings — the faster it swings, the harder it hits.
RamdozerRam buildings head-on. Push TNT crates — touching one lights a 12 s fuse.
BallistaAction fires missiles (limited — reload at crates). Alt = turbo.
J-BombHold Action to fly (fuel recharges on the ground). Alt in the air slams straight down; tap Alt on the ground (or on a roof) for a hop-stomp.
SubwayThrottle drives north, brake drives south. Stop at stations to get off.
On footWalk anywhere vehicles can't go (stairs!). Action = dive-roll.
