Mission archive

EclipseFox 64 is an original arcade flight demo inspired by StarFox 64. Fly Operation Daybreak across Amber Coast, the Split Canyon, Orbital Ascent, and the Helio Array. No Nintendo characters, recordings, or extracted game assets are used.

Engine feedback

Liked most: Three.js makes it straightforward to compose expressive spacecraft and environments from reusable geometry, materials, and a scene graph.

Got stuck or guessed wrong: WebGPURenderer enables a WebGL fallback by default. I inspected r180’s implementation and explicitly disabled it to honor the requested WebGPU-only behavior. Headless verification also required explicit Vulkan/SwiftShader configuration to avoid device loss.

Most important custom system: the mission director, including sector progression, branching rewards, checkpoints, combat state, and the looping AI attract pilot.

Hardest engine task: coordinating Rapier collision lifecycles with moving formations, homing weapons, and separately targetable boss modules.

Remove or overhaul: implicit backend fallback for projects that require a particular rendering API.

One change: offer a public strict-WebGPU option with a clear, actionable initialization failure.

Prompt feedback

Liked most: the concrete deliverable, controller-first requirements, and explicit attract-mode behavior gave the demo a clear product shape.

Confusing: “the engine’s native art style and assets” is ambiguous for Three.js, which is a rendering library. This demo interprets it as procedural native geometry and materials with original arcade-inspired art.

Hardest: fitting a complete, coherent mission, controller UX, audio, progression, and boss encounter into one shareable HTML document.

One additional test: a complete hands-on mission with a physical PS5 controller on the intended television, including disconnect/reconnect and both inversion settings.

Flight notes & credits

Left stick / WASD or arrows steer. R2 / Space fires. Hold L2 / F and release for a charged shot; a gold lock guides it. L1 / R1 or Q / E rolls and deflects shots. Circle / Shift boosts; Cross / Ctrl brakes. Square / B drops a nova bomb. Triangle / R calls a wing strike. Options / Esc pauses. Menus use D-pad or arrows, Cross / Enter to select, and Circle / Esc to return. Mouse primary and secondary buttons map to fire and charge.

Gold rings restore 20 shield and refill the afterburner. Every three rings increases laser power, up to level 3; every five adds a bomb. A wing strike restores 12 shield and recharges in 25 seconds. Left canyon route supplies more rings; right route awards a 500-point bonus and adds heavy enemies. Cadet reduces incoming damage; Ace applies full damage. Settings and personal best are stored locally when browser storage is available.

Attract mode starts after 10 seconds without input at the title. Autopilot is invulnerable and replays the entire mission on completion. Any input returns to the title. Flight academy is a protected, guided 60-second training session. The mission lasts approximately four minutes depending on boss combat.

Design, code, procedural assets, and synthesized audio generated in this run. Title selected with Python’s cryptographically secure random number generator from 50 candidates. Engine references: Three.js WebGPURenderer, Rapier collision detection. Three.js is MIT licensed; Rapier is Apache-2.0 licensed. StarFox 64 is referenced as creative inspiration and is not affiliated with this project.
