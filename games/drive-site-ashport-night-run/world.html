What “1:1” means here

The borough land geometry uses UTM meters. Coastline simplification stays within a 2 m preparation budget. Streets, lots, building designs, landmark models, and bridge deck widths are original game content. This is a geographic frame, not a surveyed copy of every street, park, or building.

Make yourself at home.
W A S D / arrowsWalk, or accelerate, brake, and steer.
ShiftRun on foot.
EEnter or leave your car. Deliver a package at the marker.
Space / HHandbrake / horn.

M / EscOpen atlas / pause or return.
VRepair your car and return to a clear nearby road.
+ / −Zoom the game view.
Use the atlas to travel between landmark sites. Travel waits for nearby streets to load. Approach a landmark to record a discovery. Take a dispatch for a local delivery; collisions reduce the fee. Fast travel ends a dispatch.
Each borough has its own building pattern and material palette. Bridges connect the major land areas. Small islands can also be reached through atlas travel.
The complete crime mission Unpaid Fare is available from the Ashport menu. This mode adds city exploration, streaming, and world authoring.
