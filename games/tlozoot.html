A small legend

Find the seed sling in the old wood. Enter the temple and restore its three ward flames.

Watch your foe. Block a strike, then answer with your sword. Roll through a wave of roots.

Gold buds react to seeds. Blue shrines restore hearts and seeds. Cut grass and break pots for supplies.

The western court holds a flute. The eastern tablet holds its song.

Gamepad: Left stick moves. Right stick turns. A rolls. X attacks. B uses. Y fires. LT locks. RT shields. LB opens the flute. D-pad and A play notes. Start pauses.

Touch controls appear on touch screens. Drag the open view to turn.
