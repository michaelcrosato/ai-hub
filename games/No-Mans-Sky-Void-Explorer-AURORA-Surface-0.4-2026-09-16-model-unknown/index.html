AURORA / FLIGHT MANUAL

Your ship. Your course.

Hold thrust to raise the throttle; release it to keep that setting. Steer with the arrows, the left stick or by dragging the sky. Select a world by clicking or tapping it, or aim at it and target. Fast transfer flies there and brakes before arrival.

KEYBOARD & MOUSE

Drift turns the ship without changing the travel direction; release it to align velocity with the nose. Use Descend after arrival to enter the atmosphere, then Land on a dry site. Open the ramp, leave the ship and walk out: every landing site hides glowing survey samples for the explorer log. Return to the ramp to board, then take off. Gear cannot retract while the ship is landed. Every transfer is continuous flight. All ranges use distance to the destination surface.
