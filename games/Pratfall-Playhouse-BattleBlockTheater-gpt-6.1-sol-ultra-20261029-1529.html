Testing Feedback

How many issues did testing catch?

8 issues, all fixed: HUD formatting; Hub probe schema; unsafe checkpoint; menu-close/dash input; secondary keyboard help; attract timing; narration obscuring hazards; renderer texture cleanup regression.

How many were critical or game breaking?

2: an unsafe checkpoint caused repeat deaths, and an over-eager texture cleanup crashed the WebGPU renderer on scene changes. Both were fixed.

Most helpful test?

Deterministic real-Rapier browser scenarios, which caught the checkpoint respawn loop and verified combat, environmental mechanics and all three act transitions.

Least helpful test?

Ordinary headless WebGPU canvas screenshots on this machine: its software compositor captured white even for a raw GPU clear. Accurate GPU output readback was used for visual review instead.

Engine Feedback

What did you like most about the engine?

Three.js scene graphs and toon materials made the layered theater and articulated cast straightforward to compose.

Did you get stuck or guess wrong?

Yes. GPU readback isolated misleading white screenshots, and final scene-change testing caught an unsafe WebGPU texture cleanup assumption.

Most important thing built yourself?

Responsive platform movement, the game-state/campaign layer, controller menus and host-authoritative co-op synchronization.

Hardest part of using the engine?

Distinguishing renderer output from headless GPU compositor behavior while keeping physics, animation and snapshots in agreement.

One thing to remove or overhaul?

Implicit WebGL fallback in WebGPURenderer; strict WebGPU requirements should be a supported public option.

One thing to change?

Provide clearer initialization/device diagnostics and an official reliable screenshot/readback helper.

Prompt Feedback

What did you like most?

The complete vertical-slice goal tied mechanics, theatrical presentation, co-op and menu polish to one coherent result.

What was confusing?

The boundary of “all features” for an original spiritual successor; I defined the complete scope as three acts covering every implemented mechanic.

What was hardest?

Making physics, multiplayer, attract mode, original audio and controller-first UX feel like one finished show in a single HTML file.

One additional test you would run?

A complete online playthrough by two people on separate physical machines using actual PS5 controllers.
