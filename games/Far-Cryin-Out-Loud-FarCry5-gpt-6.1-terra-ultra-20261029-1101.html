The fine print

About the game

FAR CRYIN’ OUT LOUD is an original playable resistance vignette inspired by Far Cry 5, not affiliated with Ubisoft. Free the captive, silence the radio tower, and hold the chapel.

Source information: 2026-10-29 11:07 · Far Cry 5 · Three.js r180 WebGPU, Rapier 0.19.3, Gamepad API, AI Hub · stylized low-poly cel-inspired art · procedural animation · synthesized music and sound effects, no voice · online: AI Hub parallel score sessions · lightweight viewport/browser testing · Codex CLI headless, Linux, browser unknown · OpenAI gpt-6.1-terra, reasoning effort ultra · usage unknown.

Testing feedback: issues caught unknown; critical issues unknown; most helpful and least helpful tests unknown.

Engine feedback: Three.js offers flexible scene composition. WebGPU and physics integration required care. Gameplay systems had to be built from scratch. Browser compatibility was hardest. WebGPU diagnostics and error messages could be improved.

Prompt feedback: The controller-first direction was useful. Shared-world versus parallel multiplayer was ambiguous. The hardest part was fitting an open-world-inspired slice in one file. An extended real-controller multiplayer playtest would be valuable.
