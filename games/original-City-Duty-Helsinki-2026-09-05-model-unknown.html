At a stop: Align with the marked bay. Stop fully. Apply B. Open F to board passengers. Open G when passengers must leave. Keep doors open until the path is clear.

Access assistance: Speak with T. Open G, lower the bus with K, then extend the ramp with R. After use, retract the ramp, close the doors, and raise the bus. Pushchairs also use the centre door with the bus lowered, without the ramp.

Departure: Check the rear views. Signal with Q. Release B. The bus cannot move with open doors, an extended ramp, or a lowered body. If someone runs for the bus, wait with the front door open.

Route: Press M for the map. Press Esc to pause. The pause menu includes recovery, lights and wipers, settings and a shift report. At the last stop, let every passenger leave, close the doors, then apply P.
