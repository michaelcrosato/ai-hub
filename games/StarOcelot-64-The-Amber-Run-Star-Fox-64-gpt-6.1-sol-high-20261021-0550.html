ENGINE FEEDBACK

Liked most: Three.js makes custom geometry, cameras, lighting and composited effects easy to combine inside one portable file.
Stuck or guessed wrong: I initially reused a changing results-button selector and let player input polling interrupt AI charge locks. Browser tests caught both; both are fixed. A file-URL path decoding mistake in the build script was also corrected.
Most important custom system: The complete flight/combat simulation, controller-first menu state machine, checkpoint director and data-driven mission sheet.
Hardest part: Balancing depth perception, aiming assistance, encounter pacing and readable targeting in a rail shooter.
Remove or overhaul: Manual ownership and cleanup of short-lived GPU resources.
One engine change: A built-in entity lifecycle and input mapping layer would make this kind of complete small game easier to author.

FLIGHT DESIGN & CREDITS

Sunskipper interceptor: you. Kite: wing lead and flight instructor. Mica: support wing. Rook: heavy wing. Your objective is the Helix carrier. The canyon route trades open firing lines for mining hazards; the orbital route changes the setting to a debris-filled salvage yard. Three gold rings upgrade your lasers, six unlock spread fire. Checkpoints restore your loadout when retried. Scores and settings are stored locally when browser storage is available.

Gamepad menus: left stick / D-pad to navigate, ✕ to select, ○ to return. Settings: left/right to adjust. Credits: right stick to scroll. Keyboard: arrows, Enter, Escape; Page Up/Down scroll these credits. Audio begins after an input gesture. This is an independent tribute; it is not affiliated with Nintendo.
