The pinned source records describe the motion data as free for all uses
and permit copying, modification, and redistribution.

Nacre uses 11 selected clips. Some passages were trimmed. Two walk
cycles were adjusted to repeat. Character art, clothes, props, motion
blending, and game-specific poses were made for Nacre.

The original source records, take intervals, and adaptation notes are
preserved with the selected motion set and the offline source package.
