SPRITE UPDATE 01 / ASSET CREDITS AND LIMITS

This version uses animation frames cut from five uploaded sheets: Rifle Soldier, Bazooka Soldier, Mini-Gun Soldier, P.O.W.s / Helpful NPCs, and Type 02 Di-Cokka. The bazooka sheet also supplies parachutes and explosions. Frames are embedded in this HTML file. No network connection is needed.

Sprite art: SNK / Playmore. Rifle, bazooka, prisoner, and Di-Cokka sheet extraction: Gussprint. Mini-gun sheet: RetroSpriteZone / Metal Slug Sprite Database. The game is an unofficial fan prototype.

The player and player-vehicle sheets available in this chat are 207 and 208 pixels wide. They do not retain enough detail for clean animation. The player, player vehicle, and final boss still use the previous game art. The title, level, music, and sound effects are also unchanged. Full-resolution player and vehicle sheets are needed to finish this change.
