Operation Monsoon Ink

Colonel Varga's militia has hijacked a tactical warhead and is moving it through the delta to a barge at the river docks.

You fly PSI-7 "Inkhawk", a lone attack gunship. Fuel, armor and ammo are all you have — scavenge drums and crates from the field. Three gunships are on standby.

Fuel · Armor · Ammo

Fuel burns constantly. Hover over drums and crates to winch them up — some are hidden inside huts.

Winch & LZ

Slow down over a pickup and the cable drops on its own. Hover over the yellow LZ pad to unload aircrew or cargo.

Threats

Shoot SAM missiles down or break sideways to dodge them. Losing a gunship respawns you at your last objective.
