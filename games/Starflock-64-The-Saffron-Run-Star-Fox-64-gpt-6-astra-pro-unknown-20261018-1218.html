The game

Starflock 64 is an original arcade flight demo. Lead Rook, Pip, and Kestrel through the Saffron coast. Choose your route, help your squad, and stop the Iron Heron.

Source

Starfox 64 for N64 / Star Fox 64 (Nintendo 64). Its rail flight, squad dialogue, route choices, charged attacks, and staged boss battles informed this demo.

Art direction

Cel-shaded aircraft, dark outlines, and stepped light. Cream wings cross a turquoise sea, terracotta rock, and amber clouds. All models and interface artwork are original.

Sound & code

Original procedural music and effects. Custom native WebGPU/WGSL renderer. JavaScript ES2022, HTML5, CSS3, SVG, Gamepad API, and Web Audio API. Version 1.0. No external libraries.

This is an independent demo. Nintendo did not make, approve, or sponsor it. Game models, scenery, characters, text, and sound were created for this file.
