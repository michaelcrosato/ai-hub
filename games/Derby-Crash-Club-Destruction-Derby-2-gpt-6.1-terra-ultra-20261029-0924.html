Testing feedback

One critical test-environment issue: the headless software WebGPU device was lost. Confirmed game-code defects: 0; 3D rendering remains unverified. Most helpful: comparing a minimal Three.js scene. Least helpful: syntax-only checking.

Engine feedback

Three.js makes procedural scenes expressive. A headless WebGPU device loss blocked visual verification; the same failure occurred in a minimal scene. The arcade vehicle, AI and race rules were built for this game. Bringing rendering, physics and networking together was hardest. WebGPU initialization is the main candidate for overhaul; clearer device-loss diagnostics would help.

Prompt feedback

The best part was the specific arcade inspiration and controller-first brief. Multiplayer scope was the least explicit. The hardest part was making the whole slice cohesive. A multi-device controller and network playtest would be the most useful additional test.
