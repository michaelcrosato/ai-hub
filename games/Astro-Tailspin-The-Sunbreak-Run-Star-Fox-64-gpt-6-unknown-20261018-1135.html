An original arcade flight

For the joy of the barrel roll.

Astro Tailspin is an original spiritual successor to Starfox 64 for N64. Meet Kestrel, Pip and Mica of Sunbreak Squadron. Their mission: stop the imperial Weathermaker from draining the archipelago’s sunlight. No original game assets are used. Not affiliated with Nintendo.

Source information
Date and time: 2026-10-18 11:35 PST
SOURCE: Starfox 64 for N64
Tech/Engine: native WebGPU / WGSL, JavaScript, HTML/CSS and Web Audio API; no external libraries.
Art direction: cel-shaded low-poly aviation; ink edges, terracotta islands, turquoise sky, cream and coral aircraft.
Environment: Codex CLI; Ubuntu 26.04 on WSL2. Target: WebGPU-capable browser. Runtime tests omitted at user request.
Model and effort: OpenAI, GPT-6 (session identification); reasoning effort unknown.

Prompt reflection: The controller-first arcade loop was a great constraint. “Unlimited performance” leaves the right amount of detail open to interpretation. The hardest part was combining combat, cinematic pacing, autonomous play and controller menus in one dependency-free file. All aircraft, landscapes, effects and synth audio are generated in code.
