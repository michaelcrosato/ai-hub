The business of busting

1. Hold the stream on a ghost to lock on and wrangle it. Swing it into walls, or slam it, for big damage.
2. When its bar turns yellow it is weak: throw a trap under it, keep the stream on and drag it over the light.
3. Watch your pack heat. Vent before it overheats; venting also blasts nearby ghosts away.
4. Invisible ghosts and hidden artifacts show up on the PKE meter. Artifacts pay a bonus.
5. Every broken chair, vase and piano is billed to the client and taken off your fee. Mind the furniture.
6. Never cross the streams. Except when you absolutely have to.
