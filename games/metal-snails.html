TACTICS

Hold Up and a direction for diagonal fire. Hold Down to crouch. Aim down in the air. Hold Jump to jump higher. Press Down + Jump to drop through a raised platform. Your knife replaces gunfire when a soldier is very close. Barrels can hurt you, so keep your distance.

Press E near the blue-green snail to board it. It has its own armor, a rapid gun, and a cannon. Press E again to leave it. A destroyed scout ejects you automatically.

GAMEPAD

Use the left stick or direction pad to move and aim. Use the bottom face button to jump, the left face button to fire, the right face button for grenades, and the top face button to board. Press Start to pause. Touch controls also support more than one finger.

BEHIND THE SHELL

A SMALL TRIBUTE TO A VERY BIG CRAFT.

Metal Snails is an independent, original one-mission arcade game. It is inspired by the run-and-gun games of the 1990s. It is not an SNK product. No SNK sprites, maps, recordings, music, or logos are included.

ART & MOTION

The game uses a 480 × 270 pixel canvas, 443 character frames, with distinct weapon models, separate vehicle and aircraft frames, 16-frame explosions, and three scrolling scenery layers. The character palettes use 15 colors with transparency. All pictures are embedded in this file. Leg cycles use eight contact and passing poses. Torso, recoil, scarf, jump, throw, knife, and death states have separate frames.

REFERENCE STUDY

The study covered the original sprites' silhouette, color groups, outlines, running poses, recoil, and death sequences. These references guided new designs rather than serving as shipped assets.

SOUND & CODE

The score is an original, browser-synthesized arrangement with FM brass, bass, drums, and a boss variation. Gunfire, impacts, explosions, and engine sounds are synthesized with Web Audio. Radio calls are generated with eSpeak and processed with a radio filter. The game uses plain JavaScript and Canvas 2D. No network connection, install, server, or account is required.

The complete source, image data, and sound data are in this HTML file. The art is a new interpretation, not a frame-for-frame copy of the original games.
