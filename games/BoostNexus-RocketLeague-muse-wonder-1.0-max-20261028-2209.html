HOW TO PLAY

Score more than the other team before the clock dies. Hit the ball into the orange goal if you're blue. That's it. Everything else is how stylishly you get there.

DRIVE
RT / W go · LT / S brake & reverse · Left stick / AD steer. Small pads give 12 boost, the big corner orbs give 100. Boost with R1 or Circle / Shift — on the ground it shoves you, in the air it flies you.
JUMP, DODGE, FLY
Cross / Space jumps. Tap again mid-air with a direction to dodge-flip — your fastest hit. Hold Square / K to powerslide, and to air-roll while airborne. Pitch with the left stick in the air. Land on all four wheels!
CAMERA & EXTRAS
Triangle / C toggles Ball Cam. Right stick / mouse drag looks around. Supersonic contact demolishes an opponent (they respawn in 3 s). Tied at zero? Golden-goal overtime. Pause with Options / Esc.
SPLIT & ONLINE TIPS
Each human gets a corner boost gauge. In split-screen the screen divides automatically. Online host: set your match up first, then Start. Guests just steer — the host's world is the truth.

SETTINGS

Saved on this machine. Axis-invert fixes live here: if your stick feels backwards, flip one switch and you're home.

ABOUT — BOOST NEXUS

Source Information
Date: 2026-10-28 22:10 · Source: Rocket League (spiritual successor, original code & art)
Tech: Three.js 0.180.0 WebGPURenderer (WebGPU only, no fallback), Rapier 0.19.3 physics, Web Audio, Gamepad API, WebRTC + BroadcastChannel.
Art: cel-shaded toon materials with inverted-hull outlines. Animation: procedural rigid-body + wheel/suspension work. Audio: fully procedural Web Audio (music, engine, hits, horn, crowd); on-screen callouts instead of voice samples.
Online: WebRTC host-authoritative + same-browser rooms, up to 4. Testing: lightweight headless smoke only. Environment: Muse Code CLI (headless); Linux; browser unknown. Model: Meta muse-wonder-1.0 (max). Usage: unknown.

Built as a complete vertical slice: kickoffs, boost economy, dodges, aerials, demolitions, golden-goal overtime, replays of the last goal in the celebration cam, three bot skills, garage customisation, split-screen to four, online, training, attract mode and a full settings suite. Fifty title riffs were brainstormed; Boost Nexus won the random draw.

Engine notes: WebGPU + TSL-era Three rewards keeping materials simple — toon shading reads beautifully at speed. The hardest build was a car controller that feels like Rocket League on top of a general physics engine: most of the feel is bespoke force shaping, not the solver.
