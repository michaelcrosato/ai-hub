How to play

Race from the start dock to Fangbite Falls. Pass every glowing checkpoint gate in order, then cross the finish arch. Your placing is against 3 rival boats.

  • Steer / Go: Left stick or A/D • R2 or W to accelerate, L2 or S to brake.
  • Fire: ✕ or SPACE. Pop target buoys (+250), stun rivals, and clear mines.
  • Boost: ○ or SHIFT — only when the yellow TURBO meter has charge. Ramps launch you over trouble.
  • Pick-ups (float & spin): yellow = turbo, pink = spread shot, cyan = shield, green = repair, gold star = +500.
  • Danger: logs, rocks, mines, bank turrets and whirlpools. Whirlpools pull and spin you — steer out or boost out.
  • Near-miss & clean gates give bonus points. Hitting banks wrecks hull — grab green repair.
