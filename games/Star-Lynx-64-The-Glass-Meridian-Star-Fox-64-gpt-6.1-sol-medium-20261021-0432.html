Hold fire while a target is inside your reticle. The amber ring fills in 0.9 seconds; release to send a seeker. Barrel rolls reflect incoming fire for 0.65 seconds. Boost uses energy; brake tightens turns.

At the canyon fork, fly left for the salvage channel or right for the storm ridge. Fly through gold rings for shields; cyan cores upgrade lasers. In all-range mode, horizontal steering turns your ship. Follow the radar to hunt enemies behind you.
