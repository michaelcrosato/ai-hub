FLIGHT SCHOOL

Small ship. Big energy.

Left stick / WASD or arrowsSteer through the flight corridor.
✕ / Space — fireHold for lasers and charge. Release a full charge to launch a seeking shot.
□ / Q or E — barrel rollBriefly deflect incoming bolts. Recharges in 2 seconds.
○ / B — nova bombClear nearby enemies and bullets. Three per sortie; collect more.
R1 / Shift — boostSurge forward. Burns engine energy.
L1 / Ctrl — airbrakeSlow the action to line up shots. Shares engine energy.
Options / Escape — pauseMenus use D-pad / arrows, ✕ / Enter, ○ / Escape.
Gold rings & teal salvageRings restore shields. Salvage upgrades lasers; three tiers.

Keep a target near the center of your sight to lock a charged shot. Orange arrows warn of incoming fire. Choose your route by flying left or right at the breach. Destroy the carrier's two orange shield relays, then its glowing core.
