FIELD MANUAL

The war in 60 seconds

🟢 Harvest: your Harvester auto-runs between green Tiber crystals and your Refinery. Crystals regrow slowly — fight for the rich centre fields.

🏗 Build: Power Plant → Refinery → Barracks / War Factory → Tech Lab. Watch ⚡ power: below zero, production crawls and turrets go dark. Place buildings near your Construction Yard (glowing pad). Select the Yard / Barracks / Factory, then pick from the right-hand dock.

⚔ Fight: select troops, then Circle / right-click ground to move, ⚔ Attack-move to advance-and-fire, Square / A for attack-move with controller. Rocket troops shred armour; rifles shred infantry; artillery out-ranges turrets but is fragile up close. Engineers capture enemy buildings (walk them into one) and repair yours.

☄ Ion Cannon: needs a Tech Lab, then charges ~90s. Pick the ready button, then pick a target. The Crimson missile is telegraphed with a red ring — move out of it.

⌨ Keys: WASD/arrows pan · wheel zoom · Q/E rotate · Left-click select, drag = box-select · Right-click smart order · A attack-move · S stop · H hold · P patrol · F all army · 1–4 control groups (Ctrl+number sets) · Space centre on alert · Esc pause/settings.

🎮 DualSense: Left stick cursor · Cross select/confirm · Circle smart order/back · Square attack-move · Triangle all-army · L1/R1 build tabs · R2/L2 zoom · Options pause · L3 HQ · R3 idle harvester. Menus: D-pad / left stick + Cross.
