Controls

Desktop. Click the view to look. WASD moves. Shift runs — running spends fatigue. Left mouse or Space strikes. C or right mouse casts. E talks, takes, and uses roads. J opens the journal. I opens the pack. R rests. Q cycles spells. Esc pauses. Arrows turn your head if you would rather not capture the mouse.

Touch. The left stick moves. Drag the right side of the screen to look. Strike, Cast, Talk, Journal, Rest, and Run sit under your thumb. Run stays down until you tap it again.

A swing that meets flesh can still miss. A cast that leaves your hand can still fizzle. Fatigue, skill, and the relevant attribute decide. Magicka returns when you rest, not when you wait. The compass shows north. It does not show your quest.
