TRAVEL NOTES

How to play

Explore: move through Bramblewick, Echo Hollow and Mossbell Orchard. The amber ring around the map shows encounter tension.

Rest: drink from the village well or ring the Mossbell to restore HP and MP. Your journey saves as you go.

Battle: choose Attack, Skill, Item, Guard, or Flee. Against two foes, pick a target. Nib acts on instinct once recruited.

Gold: Perrin's stall sells tonics, Moonleaves, a sword and a buckler.
