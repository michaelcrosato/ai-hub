How to play

Explore: move through Bramblewick and Echo Hollow. The amber ring around the map shows encounter tension.

Interact: use Space / the action button when the prompt appears.

Battle: choose Attack, Skill, Item, or Guard. Nib acts on instinct once recruited.

Camera: drag the upper half of the game view to rotate. Movement stays camera-relative.
