ENGINE FEEDBACK

Liked most
Three.js scene composition and procedural geometry made the original aircraft and environments straightforward to author.

Stuck or guessed wrong
The renderer installs an automatic WebGL fallback. Its fallback callback had to be explicitly disabled for this WebGPU-only build.

Most important custom system
The mission director: branching objectives, checkpoints, combat progression, squad support, attract mode, and results.

Hardest part
Keeping the camera, aiming reticles, kinematic flight, and Rapier projectile collisions in the same spatial model.

Remove or overhaul
Silent graphics-backend substitution in deployments that require a specific backend.

One change
A public strict-WebGPU option with a clear initialization failure would remove reliance on the internal fallback callback.

FLIGHT NOTES

A three-act approach leads to a two-phase carrier battle. Steer left or right at the canyon fork to choose your objective. Three gold rings upgrade the cannons. Rolls deflect incoming bolts; blue rings repair shields. The mission saves a checkpoint at each act and before the carrier.

Controller: left stick steers, R2 fires, L2 holds lock-on and releases a missile, L1/R1 rolls, Cross boosts, Circle brakes, Triangle launches a nova, Square calls your wingmate, Options pauses. Menus use left stick / D-pad, Cross to select, Circle to return. Scroll credits with a stick or the arrow keys.

Keyboard: WASD / arrows, Space or J to fire, Shift to charge, Q/E to roll, K/L for boost/brake, F for nova, R for wingmate, Esc to pause. Mouse buttons also fire / charge. Touch-only play requires a controller or keyboard.

Dependencies are downloaded from jsDelivr. Three.js r180 and Rapier 0.19.3 are distributed under their respective MIT and Apache-2.0 licenses. Barlow Condensed and Rajdhani fonts use the SIL Open Font License. No affiliation with Nintendo.
