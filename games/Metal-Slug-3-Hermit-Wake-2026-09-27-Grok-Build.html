CONTROLS

Keyboard, pad, touch

MOVEA D or arrows. You face the way you run, and you shoot that way. UPW or up. Aim upward. At a prompt, enter the route or vehicle. DOWNS or down. Crouch. In the air, aim down. In a tank, hunker. JUMPZ, K, or Space. Tanks and walkers jump. The sub swims. FIREX, J, or left click. Hold to shoot. Point-blank is a knife. BOMBC or right click. Grenade on foot. Cannon, lizard, or torpedo in a vehicle. BOARDF or Enter. Mount a vehicle. Bail from a tank or walker. The sub does not surface early. PAUSEEsc or P. F3 shows the diagnostics line. PADStick or d-pad moves. A jumps, X or RT fires, B or RB is the bomb, Y boards. TOUCHStick on the left half of the screen. Buttons on the right.

HERMIT WAKE — completion report
Built 27 September 2026 by Grok Build (xAI).
Engine: CO55 2D Engine v0.4.0 (MIT), inlined unchanged from the supplied single-file edition.
View: side. World x is right, z is up. One tile-step is 16 units. Gameplay uses dt from the engine's fixed 1/120 s steps.

SELECTED LEVEL
Source mission: Metal Slug 3, Mission 1, Dr. Moreau's Island / "A Couple's Love Land" (arcade / Neo Geo).
This demo is one complete cove assault with the mission's defining fork and boss, not every detour.

SOURCE CHECKLIST → WHERE IT LANDED
Beach landing, crabs, acid bubbles, destructible lair → Couple's Cove opening. Shoot the mound. Crabs hop out.
Grenade the shallows for fish → left shoreline. A bomb in the pale water pops a school.
Shoot floating debris → rocket scrap in the sky, worth a medal.
POW rescue → tied captives. Shoot or knife the ropes, then touch them.
Heavy machine gun H from the first captive.
Supply officer with a super grenade → Nami on the beach (original name; same role as the source's backpack drop).
Route fork → high jungle gate, or the Tide Mule parked past the pit.
Upper route: locusts, a jump, rebel deck, POW, then board the Iron Mule → Jungle Deck.
Lower route: swim a sub through jellyfish, mines, a heavy crab, a caged captive, a mini-sub → Tide Trench.
Both routes meet the hermit on a bridge that scrolls and collapses → Hermit Bridge.
Boss attacks: arcing fireballs, then a marked mortar. Move. The claw punishes hugging the left edge.
Alternate vehicles: Iron Mule (cannon + vulcan) from the deck; Ridge Walker (ground lizard + vulcan) from the trench. The sub is lost at the surface, as in the source.
POWs during the boss walk in from the right. Grab them before the hermit does.
One-hit soldier, armored vehicles, bail-out, weapon ammo, grenades, score, lives, continues, mission tally.
End bonuses: captives, vehicle still running, clear, and GREAT if you also kept a clean run with at least five rescues.
Van that drops a parachute bomb → one M-3 style truck on the far beach.
Flying fish in the pit can be shot for points and sometimes a bomb.
Fat gag: two rations in one life make Rico stuffed for a few seconds — slower, wider, handgun doubles.

ORIGINAL ADDITIONS (not source material)
Title Hermit Wake, pilot Rico, officer Nami, vehicle names Iron Mule / Tide Mule / Ridge Walker, boss name Hermit.
Coyote time and a jump buffer. Crouch-lob grenades. Shooting enemy grenades and bubbles out of the air.
Quality presets Auto / High / Balanced. Diagnostics line (F3). Local best score.
Checkpoint restart at the current route instead of a credit-only continue from the last street. Two continues after a wipe still restart that checkpoint with three lives.
Synthesized music and effects. See the audio manifest inside the mission script.

EXCLUDED ON PURPOSE
Full Slug Mariner tour: named eels, volcanic maze, bomber school, U-boat fleet and armored mid-boss.
Warp-gate tunnel and the sidekick chimp.
Boat-ride set piece with two parachute vans and the full hostage line on the junk.
All ten boss-bridge captives (four appear). Later missions: snow, zombies, the tower, the final.
Laser, drop shot, and enemy chaser are not in this mission's item pool. Iron lizard exists as the walker's cannon. Super grenade exists as heavy bombs.

ENGINE EXTENSIONS
None inside the engine script. The mission adds its own side-view bodies, rigs, vehicles, and platform collision in the (x, z) plane, as the API card describes for side view.
GPU lighting is initialized and falls back on its own. Side view has no ground-plane inverse, so the canvas lighter is what you see. The mission stays playable with it off or unavailable.

AUDIO
Embedded playback is original synthesis. The AUDIO_MANIFEST at the top of the mission script maps every id. Set type to "uri" and uri to a data URI to swap a clip without touching gameplay.
Placeholder rips from spriters-resource.com and zophar.net were not embedded: those arcade dumps are copyrighted and were not fetched into this offline file. The manifest is the seam for an internal swap.

HOW TO RUN
Double-click hermit-wake-grok.html, or open it from any static host. This preview serves that same file full-frame.

KNOWN ISSUES
A max-height jump on a short window sits near the top edge. You still land.
A point-blank rocket can clip Rico. That is intended.
Shield troopers block shots from the front only.
Console hooks window.__game and window.__controlsTest exist for checks. They are not a menu.
Side-view GPU lighting does not engage (the view has no ground inverse). Canvas lighting does, and the mission was played with GPU unavailable.
