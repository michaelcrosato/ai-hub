ABOUT THE FLIGHT

StarDart 64 is an original arcade flight adventure inspired by StarFox 64. All spacecraft, characters, environments and music are original, procedurally authored assets. No Nintendo assets are included.

Engine Feedback

Liked most: Three.js native geometry and materials make a cohesive procedural world practical without external art assets.

Got stuck or guessed wrong: headless Chrome acquired WebGPU but failed to capture its canvas; headed Chrome under Xvfb solved that. Creating Rapier debris during an overlap callback also proved unsafe; a reproduction confirmed the borrow error, and deferring mutations until after the query fixed it.

Most important custom system: the mission director tying flight controls, weapon targeting, squad events, branching routes, checkpoints and boss phases together.

Hardest engine work: coordinating asynchronous WebGPU setup, Rapier query/mutation rules and a fixed-step simulation with responsive controller input and UI.

Remove or overhaul: the renderer’s implicit fallback policy should be a public, explicit option for applications that require WebGPU.

One change: provide an official strict-WebGPU initialization path with concise, actionable device diagnostics.
