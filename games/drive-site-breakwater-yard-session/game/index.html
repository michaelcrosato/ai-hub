HOW TO RIDE.

01 / KEEP MOVING

The board rolls forward. Hold W to push. Use A and D to turn. Hold S to brake.

02 / LEAVE THE GROUND

Hold SPACE to crouch. Release it to ollie. A longer hold gives a higher jump. Quarterpipes launch you back into the bowl.

03 / BUILD A COMBO

In the air, press J for a flip or K for a grab. Hold a direction to change the trick. Use A or D to spin.

04 / CONNECT THE LINE

Jump toward a rail and hold L to grind. Hold X on landing for a manual. Use A and D to balance. Jump again to keep the combo.

05 / LAND IT

Finish the trick before landing. Face along your travel direction. A clean landing banks your score. A bail loses the current combo.

06 / GO FURTHER

Fill the Special meter. Press F in the air for a Harbor Flip. Repeated tricks earn fewer points. Use ramps for enough air time.

FIELD NOTES

BUILT FOR THE LINE.

Breakwater is an original arcade skating game inspired by the compact levels and connected trick systems of PlayStation skate games.

THE GAME

One complete freight yard. Ten persistent goals. Three named gaps. Two play modes. A secret route and a reward deck.

Scores, goals, and settings are saved on this device. Free Skate is for practice; it does not complete session goals.

THE CRAFT

Built on my-3D2dge v0.6.0 by Michael Crosato, used under the MIT License. Added perspective rendering, skating physics, level geometry, and a new animated skater.

Original game art, procedural sound, and music. Sunset background created with image generation. No source-game assets, skaters, or music are used.

Inspired by Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 2 (Neversoft / Activision, 2000). This project has no affiliation with the original creators.
