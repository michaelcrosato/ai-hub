How a turn feels

Move the glowing cursor with the left stick / WASD / mouse. Cross / Enter / Click selects. Circle / Esc goes back. Options / Esc opens Settings. Every menu works with D-pad + Cross.

Attract mode begins after 10 quiet seconds. Any press wakes the throne room.

Playing solo. In an AI Hub session the title is skipped and Power is reported to the hub.
