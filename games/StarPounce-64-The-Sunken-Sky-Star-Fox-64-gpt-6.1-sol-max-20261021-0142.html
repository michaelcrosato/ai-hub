The useful little details

Save a pursued wingmate for a bonus. Boss armor shrugs off fire: aim for the glowing weak points. Checkpoints keep your score and supplies. Difficulty changes damage and targeting tolerance.

StarPounce 64 — The Sunken Sky is an original spiritual successor inspired by the pace, formation flying, radio chatter, and arcade spirit of StarFox 64. All ships, characters, environments, interface, music, and sound effects in this demo are original procedural work. No original game assets are included.

Engine Feedback

Liked most: Three.js TSL makes explicit cel lighting and a fully procedural world practical without an asset pipeline. Rapier gives one collision model to ships, fire, pickups and debris.

Stuck or guessed wrong: The TSL module needed an explicit three/webgpu import-map alias. Headless Chrome initially lost its software WebGPU device; explicit Vulkan launch flags resolved the harness issue. The game precompiles its initial scene, handles device loss, and explicitly disables R180's default WebGL fallback.

Most important custom system: The data-driven mission director, with rail-to-all-range transitions, branching routes, checkpoint snapshots, boss phases, squad behavior and the autonomous attract pilot.

Hardest engine work: Coordinating a moving rail world, free-flight coordinates, camera transforms and Rapier sensor lifetimes while cleaning up GPU and physics resources between sectors.

Remove or overhaul: Make renderer fallback an explicit opt-in policy, rather than an implicit backend switch.

One engine change: Provide clearer, actionable WebGPU pipeline and device-loss diagnostics.
