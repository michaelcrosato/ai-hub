PILOT MANUAL / D–01

Keep it in the air.

To rescue, stay close to an engineer or a life raft and slow down. The beam works automatically once the jammers or radar masts are down. Your craft carries four people. Hover over the green base pad to unload, repair, and reload. Blue shots and blue map markers are friendly. Orange markers are hostile.
