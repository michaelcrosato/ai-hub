ON A PHONE
Use the left stick to fly. Hold FIRE for automatic target lock. Tap MISSILE or PULSE. Hold BOOST for speed. Turn the phone sideways for a wider view.

To rescue, stay close to an engineer and slow down. The beam works automatically after both jammers are down. Your craft carries four people. Hover over the green base pad to unload, repair, and reload. Blue shots and blue map markers are friendly. Orange markers are hostile.
