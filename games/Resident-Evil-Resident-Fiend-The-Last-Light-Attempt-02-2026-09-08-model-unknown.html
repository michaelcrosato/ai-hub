The last transmission

At 02:13, a voice called from an empty estate. You came to find the caller. The front doors locked behind you.

The west carriage gate is your way out. Its motor has no power. The lock carries the Ashcroft crest.

The keeper left a note in the library. It may explain the clock in the grand hall.

Read notes carefully. A change in the house can open a new route.

FILE 01 / THE KEEPER’S LAST ENTRY
OCTOBER 17 · 1998

Do not wind it again.

The carriage gate needs power. I left a spare ceramic fuse at the back of the library. The generator is through the winter garden.

The clock stopped when the family vanished. A quarter past nine. Not a minute before. Not a minute after.

With the power on, set the hands to that time. The crest is in the drawer below the face.

When the clock rings, do not wait in the hall.

— E. Ashcroft, Keeper

Take your time. Until you cannot.

Hold your aim still for a tighter shot. Head shots use less ammunition. You can pass an enemy instead of killing it. The knife does not need ammunition, but you must get close.

Leave a way back.

Search for supplies. Make a field dressing from an herb and alcohol in the inventory. Use the typewriter to save. The clock and generator create story checkpoints.

The mouse is captured when play starts. When mouse capture is blocked, hold a mouse button and drag to look. The arrow keys also move.
Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick looks. LT aims, RT fires, X searches, Y reloads, A pushes, B uses the knife. Hold LB to run. RB uses first aid, L3 switches the torch, R3 turns around, Back opens the inventory, Start pauses.
Touch: the left thumb moves, drag on the right to look. Push the stick all the way to run. Aim toggles.

Original code, story, models, textures, and synthesized sound. Not associated with Capcom. Contains fictional violence and horror. This single-file game uses stylized 3D graphics, not console-level photorealism.
