Engine Feedback

What did you like most about the engine?
Three.js geometry primitives, scene graph and standard materials made a consistent original polygonal world straightforward; Rapier supplied reliable sensor collision events for the flight simulation.

Did you get stuck or guess wrong?
The r180 WebGPU renderer automatically offers a WebGL fallback. I checked its source and explicitly disabled that fallback before initialization. The longer boss also required better evasive behavior in the demonstration pilot.

What was the most important thing you had to build yourself?
The flight-game framework: mission timeline, combat, controller navigation, escort logic, boss states, checkpoints, original assets, sound and attract pilot.

What was the hardest part about using the engine?
Coordinating the fixed-step Rapier simulation, interpolated presentation and edge-triggered controller actions while keeping the renderer strictly WebGPU. Graphics behavior could not be confirmed under the no-visual-testing constraint.

If you had to remove or overhaul one thing from the engine, what would that be?
Overhaul the implicit WebGL fallback so a strict WebGPU requirement can be declared through a public constructor option.

If you could change one thing about the engine, what would it be?
Provide a documented strict-WebGPU startup path with unified adapter, device-loss and dependency diagnostics.
