FLIGHT LOG 01 / 03

For the joy of the barrel roll.

Cosmic Kestrel is an original spiritual successor to Starfox 64 for N64. Kestrel, Pip and Mica of Sunbreak Squadron have one mission: stop the Weathermaker from draining their archipelago’s sunlight.

All aircraft, characters, islands, effects and synthesized audio are original and generated in code. Art direction: cel-shaded aviation, clean ink contours, terraced ochre islands, mint seafoam and sun-bleached aircraft.

Inspired by the expressive simplicity of Untitled Goose Game, Lil Gator Game, Sable, The Wind Waker and Jet Set Radio. No source-game assets are included. Not affiliated with Nintendo.

Notes from the hangar.

What I liked. The controller-first brief and autonomous arcade showcase give the whole game a clear purpose.

What was open to interpretation. The boundary of a “feature-complete demo.” This edition is a complete branching sortie: combat, rescue, hazards, upgrades, three boss phases and a debrief.

The hardest part. Keeping the flight controls, readable combat, autonomous pilot, menus and cinematic rendering coherent inside one self-contained file.

Technical references: W3C WebGPU Shading Language specification; MDN WebGPU and Gamepad API documentation.
