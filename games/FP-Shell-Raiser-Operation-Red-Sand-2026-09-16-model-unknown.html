MAP II
The buttons at the top right open the map and pause the raid.

Rescue: Shoot the prison doors. Drive near the crew to pick them up. Return them home for repair and score.

Fuel: Shoot a fuel drum, then collect its supply crate. The home pad repairs and refuels your tank.

Radar: Destroy the radar tower to slow helicopter attacks. Your cannon can hit helicopters. Auto-aim helps against them.

Mortars: Operation Iron Ford has mortar pits. An orange ring marks where a shell will land. Keep moving, or smoke them blind.
