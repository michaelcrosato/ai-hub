Engine Feedback
What I liked most: Three.js scene graphs and TSL made a consistent procedural cel-shaded world straightforward; Rapier scene queries gave projectiles precise collision tests.
Where I got stuck or guessed wrong: I initially assumed rapidly creating and removing small debris bodies would be safe in Rapier 0.19.3. Sustained simulation trapped; persistent pooled bodies resolved the observed failure.
Most important custom system: the mission director linking flight, combat, wingmate rescue, routes, boss phases, controller menus and attract-mode piloting.
Hardest part: coordinating physics-body lifecycle with projectile queries and restart cleanup while keeping the pinned engine versions.
What I would overhaul: implicit graphics-backend fallback for applications that explicitly require WebGPU.
One change I would make: a documented public strict-WebGPU mode that rejects initialization without installing a fallback callback.
