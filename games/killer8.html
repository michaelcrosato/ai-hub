KILLER8 / THE VACANT FLOOR

A FAMILIAR BAD DREAM.

This is an unofficial, original browser-game homage to the art and play of Killer7. The tower mission takes its main cues from the Celtic Building opening. It does not use the original game's art, music, code, characters, or dialogue.

Built as one self-contained HTML file. Custom WebGL renderer with a software fallback. Procedural 3D art.
Original sound made in Web Audio. No network connection or install is needed.

Killer7 belongs to its respective owners. This project is not linked to or approved by them.
Contains stylized violence, sudden sounds, and flashing effects. Reduced flashes are available in Settings.
