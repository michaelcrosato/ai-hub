Flock systems / Pilot handbook

Welcome to the Reach.

Keep moving. Aim at the enemy. Collect salvage to improve your ship. Follow the gold beacon to your next target.

WASD Move your ship
MOUSE Aim · Hold click to fire
SHIFT Warp · Uses drive energy
SPACE Nova · Uses one pod
Q Scan · Pull in salvage
E Dock at Haven
R Repair · Uses one pod
F Turn auto-fire on or off
M Open the sector map
ESC Pause or close a screen

Your shield returns after four seconds without a hit. Dock for free repairs and pods. Use a scan near the friendly ship to recruit your wingmate. Destroy the three swarm relays to expose the Warden.

KEYBOARD ONLY: Arrow keys move. J fires toward the nearest enemy, or in the direction of travel.

GAMEPAD: Left stick moves · Right stick aims · RT fires · LT warps · A uses nova · X scans · B repairs · Y docks · Start pauses. On the title screen, use the D-pad to select a ship, A to launch, or Y to continue.

TOUCH: Left stick moves. Right stick aims and fires. Use the round buttons for actions.

A sortie takes about 5–8 minutes. Progress saves at docks and mission checkpoints when local storage is available.

Starflock: The Verdant Reach is an original space combat game inspired by Armada for the Dreamcast. It follows the flight from a safe station into hostile space, with ship growth, salvage, wing support, and a carrier battle.

All ship art, worlds, effects, music, and sound in this file are made with code. No original game assets are used. This is an independent project, not an official sequel.
