MERCY COUNTY / FIELD NOTES

A little local knowledge.

INDEPENDENT / ORIGINAL / UNTAMED

Made for the long way home.

FAR WILDER is an original, independent spiritual successor inspired by Far Cry 5. All county names, characters, dialogue, geometry, music and sound effects in this demo are original and generated in code. No source-game assets are included. Title selected from 50 candidates with Python’s cryptographic random generator.

Source Information

Date: 2026-10-29 15:30, America/Vancouver.
Source: Far Cry 5.
Model: OpenAI, gpt-6.1-sol, reasoning effort high.
Environment: Codex CLI (headless); Linux; browser unknown at authoring.

Tech: Three.js r180 / 0.180.0, WebGPU only; Rapier 0.19.3; Gamepad API; HTML/CSS; Canvas 2D interface; AI Hub Session Protocol v1.

Art: cel-shaded, faceted autumn wilderness.
Animation: procedural joint rigs, recoil, reload, gaits, wind and scripted attract replay.
Audio: original synthesized Web Audio score and effects; optional browser speech synthesis radio voices (voice unknown); captions.

Online: AI Hub parallel, 1–4 players, seeded eight-minute liberation challenge with progress and results. Each player plays their own county. No chat or shared-world co-op.

Usage: API cost unknown; tokens unknown. Measured session time: approximately 28 minutes.

Testing Feedback

Testing and review caught 9 issues, including 1 game-breaking shooting lookup; all were fixed. 23 gameplay checks, 24 AI Hub controller conformance checks and 8 checkpoint/traversal/layout checks passed. Most helpful: the integrated combat-to-victory run. Least helpful: syntax-only checking, which missed runtime bugs. Viewports: 320×568 to 2560×1080, plus iPhone SE and iPad Mini. Chromium 153 rendered WebGPU through SwiftShader. WebKit 26.6 and Firefox 155 verified the WebGPU requirement message and menus; gameplay was unavailable in those headless runners. Real iOS Safari and physical controllers were not available. Screenshots were inspected manually.

Engine Feedback

Liked most: Three.js scene composition and Rapier’s collision queries.
Stuck or guessed wrong: Initially used an unsupported MeshToonMaterial flatShading option; explicit faceted geometry replaced it. WebGPU also needs asynchronous initialization and an explicit fallback opt-out.
Built ourselves: mission state, enemy perception, controller UI, weapons, companion and world generation.
Hardest: integrating renderer, physics and input into one portable document.
Overhaul: replace implicit fallback with a public opt-out.
One change: built-in cel outlines and game character setup.

Prompt Feedback

Liked most: the complete slice and controller-first design mandate.
Confusing: “all features” has no finite boundary for a large open-world source. This demo defines a complete rescue-to-broadcast operation.
Hardest: cohesive systemic gameplay and single-file delivery.
One extra test: two physical DualSense controllers on separate GPU devices in a live Hub session.

Thanks for raising a little hell.
