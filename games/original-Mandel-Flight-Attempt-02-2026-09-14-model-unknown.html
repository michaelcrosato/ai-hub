Flight controls

Press Enter to capture the mouse. Use the mouse to look and WASD to fly. Press Escape to use the controls again.

With “Build as I fly” on, moving toward the terrain opens a smaller fractal region and builds finer cubes. Moving back builds a wider region. Moving sideways extends the terrain. Center keeps the current region and zoom depth.

Expeditions hide a gold beacon deeper in the set. Fly toward it, dive at it until you reach the target depth, and beat the par time. Save places you like; they stay in the location list, and Copy link shares one.

The old cubes stay visible during a build. Flight can slow at the surface while a new region builds. The Mandelbrot set is two-dimensional; cube height is a visual measure of escape time, not a Mandelbulb. Zoom stops at a safe limit for double-precision numbers.
