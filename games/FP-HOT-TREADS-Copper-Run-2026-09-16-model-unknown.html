GOOD TO KNOW

Destroy the two prisoner cages. Stop near the crew to pick them up. Return them to the green bay for points and one extra tank per four crew rescued. Cargo space depends on your tank.

Stay in the green bay to repair, refuel, and reload supplies. Shoot fuel tanks to get fuel. Smoke hides you while you stay in the cloud. The cloud lasts five seconds. Mines damage the enemy, not your tank. You can drive over enemy foot soldiers. When fuel is empty, reserve power lets you crawl home.

Keep moving. Enemy helicopters hunt tanks that stay still outside the base. Shoot them down with your cannon. Destroy the red radio station to stop new helicopter calls.

TOUCH CONTROLS

Use the left stick to drive. Use the right stick to aim and fire. The sticks appear under your thumbs. FIRE shoots with auto aim. Tap the mine or smoke button. Landscape gives you more room. In Classic mode, W/S drive and A/D turn. Sticks always use direct steering.

GAMEPAD

Left stick or D-pad drives. Right stick aims and fires. A or RT fires with auto aim. X places a mine, Y releases smoke. Back opens the map and Start pauses. In menus, the D-pad moves, A selects and B goes back.

Inspired by Fire Power for PC (1988), by Silent Software / MicroIllusions. This is an original tribute. No original game art, music, or code is used. It runs on three.js with WebGPU, or WebGL2 where WebGPU is missing, and needs no network. Best scores and settings stay in this browser.
