Credits &
details.

Rocket Ruckus is an original arcade vehicle-soccer demo inspired by Rocket League. Built with procedural art and a whole lot of boost.

Source information

Date: 2026-10-29 00:28 · Source: Rocket League
Tech: Three.js r180 WebGPU, Rapier 3D 0.19.3, PeerJS 1.5.5 / WebRTC, Web Audio, Gamepad API, HTML/CSS/JavaScript.
Art: procedural cel-shaded stadium. Animation: physics and procedural motion. Audio: synthesized music and effects; no voice. Online: private peer-to-peer rooms, up to four players. Testing: syntax, headless Chrome 154 launch, match clock, four-view layout, pause and room creation; visual WebGPU output could not be verified here.
Environment: Codex CLI (headless); Linux; Chrome 154 (headless). Model: OpenAI gpt-6.1-terra, xhigh. Usage: unknown.
