KEYBOARD

Brake before the turn. Release the brake, steer to the inside, then add throttle as the road opens. Hard steering at high speed will use up tire grip. Hold the brake at a stop to reverse. Hold the handbrake through a hairpin to swing the tail.

Gamepad: Left stick or D-pad to steer. RT or A to accelerate. LT or B to brake. X for the handbrake. Y to change the camera. LB / RB to shift. Back returns the car to the track. Start to pause.

Time Trial: Chase your best-lap ghost. Each circuit keeps its own records and ghosts in this browser. A replay is available after each full race.

Original cars, tracks, art, and music. Inspired by the driving games of the PlayStation era. No game files or network connection are needed.
