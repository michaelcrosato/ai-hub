Engine feedback

Canvas gives direct control and simple single-file delivery. No blocking engine issue was encountered. The projection, flight simulation, combat, mission tools, audio and UI were built here. Depth ordering and aiming readability were the hardest parts. For production, replace primitive sorting with a depth-buffered renderer. A standard retained 3D scene API would simplify authoring.

Prompt feedback / 404299fcd4

The controller-first direction and attract-mode requirement gave the demo a clear shape. “Unlimited performance” leaves target hardware unknown. The hardest part was fitting a complete mission and readable progression into one file. One additional test would be a complete mission on a physical PS5 controller.

Flight manual

Three mission sectors culminate in a reactor boss. Upper route: more enemies, 1.5× score. Rings repair 16 shield and replenish engines. Lock for 0.65 seconds, release L2 / Shift to fire. Roll reflects bolts for 0.8 seconds. Nova bombs have limited stock; 12 kills replenish one. Lost wingmates stop assisting. Practice difficulty reduces incoming damage. Restart resets the mission; best score and settings persist locally.
