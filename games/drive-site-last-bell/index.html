HOW TO PLAY

01Watch Rook

His shoulder and glove reveal the punch. Wait. Move near the end of the cue.

02Make him miss

Slip away from straight punches. Duck the wide sweep. A body drive needs a slip.

03Take your turn

Attack the open target. A clean combo fills FOCUS and BREAK. Use Break Punch when FOCUS is full.

Use the left thumb to slip, duck, or hold Guard. Use the right thumb to punch the head or body. Tap Break Punch when FOCUS is full.

ABOUT LAST BELL

One ring. One opponent. Three changes of rhythm.

Last Bell is an original spiritual successor to the timing and counterplay of Super Punch-Out!! for the SNES. All fighters, art, setting, music, and attack patterns in this game are new.

Engine: my-3D2dge Agent Edition v0.12.1 by Michael Crosato. Used under the MIT License, with display and input changes.

Art: Original generated illustrations, with custom animation, effects, and sprite integration. Sound: Original procedural score and effects. Type: Nimbus Sans Narrow by URW, used under its font license.

Research: Nintendo’s official manual and StrategyWiki’s SNES guide.

This is an independent game. It has no affiliation with Nintendo.
