Time counts only while the van moves. Results accumulate for each mode. Course columns show the best result with each mode. Your settings, notes, and ratings stay in this browser when local storage is available. Export the log to keep a separate copy.

The thumb wheel

Touch the rim. Move your thumb in a circle around the centre. Clockwise turns right. You can touch any part of the rim without a jump. Release and move your thumb to a new point to continue the turn.

At the default gain, a 90° thumb turn gives full steering input. The touch wheel shows your input. The small wheel in the cab shows the steering shaft angle. Speed assistance reduces the front wheel angle at high speed.

A complete drive system in every mode

GAS accelerates. BRAKE slows the van and overrides gas. Release both to coast. Select D to drive forward, R to reverse, or N for neutral. Stop before changing between D and R. P is the parking brake. There is no automatic creep.

In Slide pedal mode, touch higher on a pedal for more input. In Cruise mode, tap GAS to hold the set speed. Brake, change gear, or open the menu to cancel cruise. The one-thumb pad and wheel + throttle modes also control gas and brake, but the separate pedals remain available.

Look, reverse, and reset

Drag the clear windscreen to look left or right. Release to look ahead. Hold REAR to look behind. RESET puts the van on the starting road. It does not erase your test log.

Test courses

Open Courses to pick a test. Practice loop: six gates around the block, timed per lap. Slalom: cross the start line north of the starting road, then weave through eight offset cone gates; a cone costs 2 s and a missed gate 5 s. Depot parking: the depot is the yard just west of the starting road. Stop with the whole van inside the lit bay, lined up within 8°, then set P. Nose in or reverse in. Each test keeps a best result for every steering mode.

Tilt steering

Select Tilt phone, then tap Enable tilt. Hold the phone in your driving position and set its centre. Keep the screen partly upright, not flat. Set the centre again after you turn the phone between portrait and landscape.

Tilt needs motion sensor access. Some browsers allow sensors only on HTTPS pages. Touch controls do not need sensors.

Compare controls

Try each mode on the same route and at a similar speed. Use Feel & layout to change gain, return speed, precision, smoothing, pedal type, and hand position. Turn on Keep steering on release to test a wheel that does not spring back.

The 900° wheel needs several thumb movements for full lock. Tap gas for cruise can free both hands for this test. Brake is always available. Use the test log to rate each mode and write notes.

Keyboard and mouse

W / ↑ Gas. S / ↓ / Space Brake. A D / ← → Steer. Q Reverse. E Drive. N Neutral. P Parking brake. F Cruise. X Centre steering. Hold V to look back. R Reset. C Next mode. M Sound. Esc Menu. F9 Graphics. Drag the windscreen to look around. The on-screen wheel and pedals also work with a mouse. Keys use the selected mode's feel settings and test log.

Gamepad

Left stick or D-pad steers. RT or A is gas. LT or B brakes. RB / LB shift between R, N and D. X is the parking brake. Hold Y to look back. The right stick looks around. D-pad up sets cruise. D-pad down or a right-stick click centres the steering. View resets. Start opens this menu. In menus the D-pad moves, A selects, B closes, LB / RB change page and the right stick scrolls.

About the test

This is a control test, not a training simulator. The van uses a 3.8 m wheelbase, a 34° low-speed steering limit, automatic gears, speed-dependent steering, and a simplified grip limit. Buildings stop the van. Sidewalks add drag. There are no pedestrians, traffic, or purchases. Course times are there to compare controls.

The game runs on three.js r186: WebGPU where the browser has it, WebGL2 otherwise. Nothing loads from outside this site and nothing is tracked. The game pauses when the page loses focus.
