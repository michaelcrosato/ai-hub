Controls & combat help

Six weapon families are in your starting kit. Press I, select a weapon, then Equip. Left click or J attacks; hold to repeat. Space cancels any attack. Q still reflects shots when using the bow.

Stone blocks water-borne electricity, not physical attacks. Your own arc can shock you. Dash through attacks, or use a water pulse to interrupt a windup.

Walk near gold pouches and non-weapon gear to collect them. Weapons stay on the ground: C compares, E takes the selected weapon, and R selects the next nearby weapon. Taking a weapon stores it; I opens equipment.

At the sanctuary, trade with salvage and unlock upgrades with total XP. Retrying restores the floor-entry kit and keeps earlier floors. Press M for sound. P also pauses. Middle click also dashes. On a phone, turn sideways for a wider view.

Equipment rules

Stored gear gives no bonuses. Empty slots use base stats. Equipping a replacement returns the old item to the pack.

Higher health capacity does not heal. Stone stays safe from water-borne electricity; gear does not change electrical damage. The red cape stays with you.

Retries restore the floor-entry kit. Completed-floor gear and sanctuary choices carry forward. Place stored items in the dungeon, or sell them at the sanctuary.
