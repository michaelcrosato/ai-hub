FIELD MANUAL / 01

STAY OUT OF SIGHT.

Approach a guard from behind. Press E at close range for a silent takedown. A tranquilizer shot puts a guard to sleep. Sleeping guards can wake up or be found. Every guard carries a dog tag: take it while he sleeps. You can complete the mission without firing.

Movement is screen-aligned. North is up. Hold E to transfer data. Cover blocks sight and shots. Running makes sound. Guards search the last place they saw you. A box does not hide you after a guard sees you enter it.
