TERRAIN PALETTE
How this tool works

Generate a world, then click the map to inspect a tile. Scroll to zoom; drag to pan. With the map focused, arrow keys pan, plus and minus zoom, Enter inspects the center, and Space applies the brush to the selected tile. Choose Paint to edit terrain at close range.

Deploy at a selected land tile to start a frontier expedition. It builds a safe camp and three connected relay routes in that small region.

Save stores the world on this device. Export JSON preserves the seed, edits, and expedition checkpoint. The story chapter keeps its own save.

World extent describes possible tiles. Only nearby exploration chunks are kept in memory.
