Procedural Open World

A low-poly streamed world with mountains, forests, plains, canyon terrain, marshland, rivers and lakes.

This is a framework: world generation, chunk streaming, biome decoration, controls, lighting, shadows and water are separated into small systems so later gameplay can be added without rewriting the base.

Expedition: waystones stand in the wild, one in every kind of land. Their light shows on the horizon and the compass points the way.
