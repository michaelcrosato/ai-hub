A FEW SIMPLE CONTROLS

Make. Move. Repeat.

The lab works offline. No account or install is needed.

Grab and inspectDrag a body to move it. Pause to place it. Click a body to change its mass or position.
Push and pulseDrag from a body to aim a push. A click pushes it away from the view. Pulse moves nearby bodies away from a point. Fixed bodies do not move.
Link and pinClick two objects to link their centers. Fixed objects use the point you click. Rope can go slack. Rod keeps a fixed length.
Build a pathSelect Draw a domino path. Drag across the floor. Release to build it. Pin keeps a structure in place.
Rewind timeMove the time slider to pause and review the last 12 seconds. Resume from the past to start a new path.
Save and openSave creates a JSON file. Open restores objects, links, world settings, and the view. Undo restores an earlier edit.
