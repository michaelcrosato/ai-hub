EXPEDITION / FIELD GUIDE

Know your equipment.

Move

Survey

Begin with a survey pulse.

Use Q to reveal nearby specimens. Look at a target, then hold E to record it. Plants, wildlife, water, and terrain appear in your journal. Use B to mark a site.

Field commissions.

The journal lists eight commissions: species to find, landmark cairns to reach, a night survey and the Crown Ridge climb. Each one unlocks equipment, like a faster scanner lens, trail boots or a brighter lamp. Some wildlife only comes out after dark: rest at the field station or a reached cairn from the pause menu to wait for nightfall.

Developer mode

Press F3 or use the pause menu. Inspect rendering data, change the time, fly, view the terrain mesh, and move to any landmark. Press F6 to toggle flight after developer mode is active.

If mouse capture is blocked, hold a mouse button and drag to look. Gamepads and touch screens work too.
