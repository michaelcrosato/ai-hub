HOW TO FLY

Left stick / WASD or arrows: move and aim. R2 / Space: rapid lasers. Hold ✕ / J to charge a tracking shot; release after the reticle turns amber. L1 or R1 / Q or E: barrel roll, briefly reflecting hostile shots. R2 can remain held during a roll. R1’s roll turns right; L1’s turns left.

R2 = fire · ✕ = charge · □ / B = nova bomb · L2 / Shift = boost · ○ / Ctrl = brake · △ / F = wingmate support · Options / Esc = pause. Use the D-pad or stick and ✕ to navigate menus. Mouse clicks can also select menus.

Fly through cyan supply rings to repair shields and upgrade twin lasers. Destroy marked pursuers to save your wingmates. At the canyon fork, your horizontal position chooses the route: LEFT refinery (more enemies, score) or RIGHT ridge (more supplies). Both lead to the carrier. Break its two engine pods, then the exposed core. Boost spends energy; brake slows approaching threats. Rolling costs energy and reflects shots. Nova bombs clear hostile fire. Wingmate support recharges in 22 seconds. A complete sortie lasts about 3–4 minutes.
