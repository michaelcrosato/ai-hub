L STICK / WASD

Fly & aim

Strafe on the coast; steer in free flight. Right stick adjusts your aim. Arrow keys also fly.

R2 / SPACE

Pulse lasers

Hold for continuous fire. Soft aim assist catches nearby targets. Three gold rings upgrade your guns.

L2 / SHIFT

Charge & lock

Hold until orange brackets appear, then release. Homing bursts damage clustered enemies.

L1 · R1 / Q · E

Barrel roll

Tap to spin and reflect red shots. Uses engine energy. Deflections earn a bonus.

× · □ / C · V

Boost & brake

Hold to change speed. Both spend energy. △ / U makes a U-turn in free flight.

○ / X

Nova bomb

Clear a wide area and erase hostile shots. Carry up to five. Options / Esc pauses.

Silver rings repair shields. Gold rings upgrade your lasers. Keep kills close together for an ×8 chain. Destroy marked hunters to rescue wingmates. Checkpoints restore your squad. D-pad navigates menus; right stick scrolls credits.

Source information

Astro Fox 64 is an original spiritual successor inspired by StarFox 64. No source assets, characters, or audio are used. The name was randomly selected from 50 candidates.

Date: 2026-10-21 03:38 America/Vancouver.
Tech: Three.js 0.180.0 / r180; WebGPU only; TSL bloom; Rapier 3D compat 0.19.3; Web Audio; Gamepad API; HTML/CSS/JavaScript.
Art: Original procedural faceted aerospace; ivory, cobalt, coral and turquoise.
Animation: Damped transforms, procedural banking, mission choreography, particles and Rapier debris.
Environment: Codex CLI; Ubuntu 26.04 on WSL2 (provided); browser: unknown.
Model: OpenAI; requested gpt-6.1-sol; xhigh. Actual backend model: unknown.

Testing: 2 syntax checks; 16 browser sessions including 6 full mission simulations; 1 bundle/report integrity check. Four game issues fixed during development: premature carrier damage, debris bounds, checkpoint progress restoration, and double Enter activation. Final simulation: no JavaScript errors; all mission, boss, death/retry, upgrade, rescue, menu, axis-toggle and attract-exit assertions passed. One critical environment limitation: WebGPU device loss also reproduced with a minimal cube, so native visual verification is incomplete. Physical PS5 controller testing not performed. Test cost: unknown.
Usage: Usage: Session time 44m 42s; tokens 7,183,507 total (7,076,854 input including 6,911,232 cached and 0 cache-write; 106,653 output including 27,941 reasoning); API cost $2.088897 USD (Estimated standard API token cost; not subscription billing).
