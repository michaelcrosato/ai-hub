Field manual

Hold fire for twin lasers. Hold charge while facing a target until the diamond turns orange, then release to fire a homing missile. Nova bombs clear nearby fire and damage the whole enemy formation. Barrel rolls grant a short deflection window. Boost and brake share regenerating energy. A squad strike attacks every visible enemy, recharging over 25 seconds. Save your wingmates when they call for help; surviving squadmates add to your mission score. At the fork, steer left for a ring-rich canyon or right for harder open-sea combat. The boss has two armored engines: destroy both before its core opens. Mission time pauses during boss combat. Your best score and settings are saved locally.
