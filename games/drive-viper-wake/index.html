KNOW YOUR RIVER.

01 · Race the rapids

Reach the finish gate. Avoid rocks, mines, and whirlpools. Use ramps to jump over hazards.

02 · Rescue the swimmers

Touch a swimmer to tow them. Bring them to the green dock. Save four. Shoot raiders to stop them.

03 · Fight the serpent

Hold fire and keep moving. The gun aims at nearby enemies. Avoid orange attack marks.

Pods & checkpoints

G gives a gun upgrade. + repairs the hull. P adds a pulse. You have three boats. Each sector is a checkpoint.
