OH–01 / FLIGHT GUIDE

ONE ARM. ALL YOU NEED.

Reach the city gate. Destroy Warden to finish the shift. Clear Raid 01 to unlock overtime in the freight yards.

TAKE THEIR GUNS. Fly near a floating gunpod to collect it. Pods have limited rounds. Your main gun never runs out.
CHANGE THE ANGLE. The lower mount fires straight. The upper mount spreads or raises the shots.
STAY IN CONTROL. Your small centre light is the hit area. Hold slow to see it. A pulse clears bullets and gives a short shield.
Mouse or touch: hold and drag in the flight area to move and fire.
