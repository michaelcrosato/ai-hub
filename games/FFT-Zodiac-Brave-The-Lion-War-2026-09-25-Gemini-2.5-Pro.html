How to Play

Keyboard & mouse

Click a command, then a tile
Arrows / WASD cursor & menus
Enter / Space confirm
1–5 Move · Attack · Skill · Item · Wait
Esc / right-click / K cancel
K on the command menu: scout the field
Q / E rotate · wheel, + − zoom
Drag pan · C recenter · P pause · H help

Gamepad

D-pad / left stick cursor & menus
A confirm · B cancel
B on the command menu: scout the field
LB / RB rotate camera
LT / RT zoom · right stick pan
RS click recenter
Start pause · Back / View help

Touch

Tap a command
Tap a tile to preview it, tap again (or Confirm) to act

↺ ↻ rotate · ☰ pause menu
  • CT: every unit's Charge Time fills by its Speed; at 100 it acts. Moving and acting resets CT to 0, doing only one to 20, waiting to 40 — waiting brings your next turn sooner.
  • Facing: attacks from the side gain +12% hit, from behind +25%. Choose your final facing well.
  • Height: archers deal +3 damage per level above their target; JMP limits how high a unit can climb. Bog water costs 2 movement.
  • Scouting: hover, tap or move the cursor onto any other unit to tint the tiles it can reach — plan around enemy charges. On your turn, B (gamepad) or K frees the cursor from the command menu.
  • Skills cost MP. Rend Armor breaks a target's armor (+30% damage taken). Alicia knows Cure.
  • Items are restocked before every battle: 4 Potions and 2 Phoenix Downs. A Phoenix Down revives a fallen ally.
  • Units earn EXP for every action and level up at 100. Progress saves after each battle.
