RAINCHECK / PLAYABLE PILOT

A small street. A long story.

This is an unofficial fan-made story set in the world of Blade Runner. Mara and the cast are new characters. There are no film clips, copied models, voice samples, or licensed music.

This build contains one complete short case and a small side case. It is not a full one-hour episode. The series plan is for twenty episodes, each built around a person, a place, and a cost.

The 3D view runs on three.js with WebGPU, falling back to WebGL2 where WebGPU is missing. Everything loads from this site. Sound starts only when you turn it on.

Choices and notes stay on this device. There are no accounts, tracking, or paid services. Use the notebook to export your save.
