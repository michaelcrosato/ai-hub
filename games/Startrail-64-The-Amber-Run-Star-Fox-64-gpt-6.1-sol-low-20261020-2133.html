Hold fire for 0.8 seconds near a target, then release for a homing seeker. Ordinary lasers keep firing while charging. A successful roll deflects hostile bolts and restores a little energy.

Gold salvage rings restore 16 shield and award a laser upgrade every three collected. Keep kills flowing to raise your score multiplier. At the fork, your ship's position chooses the route. The Dredger’s two orange engines must be destroyed before its core opens.
