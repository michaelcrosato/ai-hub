HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Goal: A driverless fertilizer carrier trundles down the farm road and will not stop. Demolish every RED blocker on its route before it crashes into one. Guide it home by clearing the road to the finish barn.

🚜 Vehicles: Walk the yard with your machine — drive up to a parked vehicle and press E / △ to swap. Ram Tractor is quick, Dozer hits twice as hard, Wrecker swings a wrecking ball with SPACE / □.

💥 Demolition: Ram at speed to damage buildings. Chain hits for COMBO ×2–×5. Bonus hay bales and corn crates add score but do not block the carrier.

🕹 PS5: Left stick steer/drive • R2 or ✕ accelerate • ○ brake/reverse • △ swap • □ swing • OPTIONS pause • D-pad menus
⌨ Keyboard: WASD/arrows drive • E swap • SPACE swing/action • P/Esc pause • Enter confirm
