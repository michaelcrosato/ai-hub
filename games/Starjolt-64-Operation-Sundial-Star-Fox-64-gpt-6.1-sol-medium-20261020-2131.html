BEHIND THE SIGNAL

Starjolt 64 — a spiritual successor inspired by Starfox 64. Original ships, characters, mission, scenery, and synthesized sound. No source game assets.

Engine feedback

Three.js's scene graph and WebGPU material mapping made procedural staging expressive. Source inspection was needed to disable automatic WebGL fallback. The mission director, flight model, targeting, controller UI, combat and audio were built for this demo. Coordinating async GPU initialization was the hardest integration. I would replace implicit fallback with an explicit policy, and add a documented strict-WebGPU flag.

Prompt feedback · 05cae7167f

The controls, attract mode and delivery contract were useful constraints. The Ground Zeroes comparison left content breadth open-ended. The hardest part was unifying combat, progression, accessibility and presentation into a complete slice. One additional test: extended physical DualSense play on several WebGPU GPUs.
