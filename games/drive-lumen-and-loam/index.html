Your covenant

Ash and Mira share vitality and light. Your companion follows, fights, and cannot be stranded behind a wall.

Let the strike meter recover for full damage. Hold a strike to charge a wider, stronger blow. A small diamond above your feet shows full charge.

Hits restore light. Fully readied hits also build your covenant. At 100%, Bloomwake damages nearby foes and heals the party.

Read the ground. Hatched amber shapes warn of attacks. Dodge during the warning; punish a guardian while its core is exposed.

Find both court beacons. Defeat their keepers, then kindle them. The eastern orchard holds an optional permanent upgrade.
